Strategy Comments

Rillaboom's role is as a bulky support Pokemon that can still do great damage. Pivot with U-turn to cycle Fake Out, giving its partner free turns, and, when possible, nuke foes with powerful Wood Hammers and Grassy Glides.

Rillaboom is usually a valuable part of the team throughout a game. In the beginning, Rillaboom is able to leverage Fake Out to allow its partner to set up early or get early damage off. Rillaboom usually pivots during the middle of the game to get more use out of Fake Out and get Grassy Terrain back on the field. Rillaboom chips down the opposing team throughout the game, but it excels at cleaning up in endgames thanks to its access to priority moves and high damage output.

The biggest checks to Rillaboom are Pokemon that resist Grass. When playing against these Pokemon, it is important to pivot out with U-turn to preserve Rillaboom and allow it to get more value later in the game with its attacks. Additionally, partners such as the aforementioned Sneasler and Primarina can help against these Grass-resistant foes.

Overview

: As the strongest Terrain setter in the format, Rillaboom is coveted for the support it can provide to a team as well as its individual strengths. Thanks to a high Attack stat and strong terrain-boosted STAB attacks—including a priority move—Rillaboom can break down many walls and threaten to sweep teams as well. On top of this, it has solid utility options and can be a good pivot in the matchups where it may struggle to wallbreak. Despite these strengths, solid but unremarkable stats outside Attack, as well as the inability to threaten some common typings, means Rillaboom should not be picked too early.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Rillaboom can use its strong Terrain-boosted STAB attacks in conjunction with damage-boosting items, such as Choice Band, to wallbreak with consistency. Grassy Glide also allows Rillaboom to break through faster offensive checks and setup sweepers with ease.

Bulky Utility Pivot

: Rillaboom may take a more defensive and supportive role that focuses on its very respectable bulk and strong utility move options, such as Knock Off, U-turn, and Leech Seed, to disrupt foes.

Setup Sweeper

: Rillaboom can use its setup options—Swords Dance and Bulk Up—to break through Pokemon that would typically check or wall to Rillaboom and secure KOs. Rillaboom can also clean teams after setting up with its strong priority Grassy Glide.

Setup Moves

: Swords Dance, Bulk Up

Utility Moves

: Knock Off, U-turn, Leech Seed, Taunt, Substitute, Protect, Brick Break

Coverage

: High Horsepower, Low Kick, Drain Punch, Hammer Arm

Niche Moves

Trailblaze

: Trailblaze can be used on setup variants to boost Rillaboom's mediocre Speed, creating more sweeping potential.

Bullet Seed

Bullet seed can be a viable STAB attack for Rillaboom if it may be benefited by a multi-hit and non-contact Grass attack.

Common Items

Choice Band

: Choice Band allows a wallbreaking Rillaboom to repeatedly dish out strong STAB attacks that even most resistances cannot weather.

Miracle Seed / Life Orb

: These damage-boosting items can help wallbreaking and sweeping variants thrive, as they allow more flexibility to set up and switch attacks.

Assault Vest

: Assault Vest lets Rillaboom take more advantage of its strong STAB attacks, useful options Knock Off and U-turn, and coverage.

Leftovers

: Leftovers will often be found on bulkier utility oriented sets that wish to check foes and disrupt the opposing team with increased longevity.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Against entry hazard stacking teams, Heavy-Duty Boots prevents hazards from weakening Rillaboom too severely. This is especially useful on sets that will frequently be pivoting with U-turn.

Niche Items

Protective Pads

: Protective Pads can be incredibly useful in order to avoid contact against Rocky Helmet users. Rillaboom can freely use contact moves against teams featuring Pokemon with abilities such as Flame Body and Static as well.

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf helps Rillaboom overcome its mediocre Speed stat, enabling it to dish out more strong attacks and serve as a fast pivot. This is preferable to damage-boosting items, as you can attack first with stronger moves compared to Grassy Glide and less Terrain-reliant.

Terrain Extender

: Terrain Extender can provide extra turns in Grassy Terrain to further support Rillaboom’s teammates.

Rocky Helmet

: A Rocky Helmet can find use on bulkier sets, allowing Rillaboom to punish contact moves.

Resistance Berries

: In a pinch, Resistance Berries such as Occa Berry and Coba Berry can allow Rillaboom to unexpectedly survive an attack, gaining an extra turn to setup or damage a foe.

Grassy Seed

: Grassy Seed can provide Rillaboom a boost to its Defense, allowing it to check a certain threat or set up with increased ease. This item also boosts Acrobatics to full power upon consumption.

Tera

Although not ideally your Tera Captain due to a plethora of more effective higher tier Tera Captains, Rillaboom can use Tera effectively in several ways. Rillaboom can use Tera Grass to obliterate most walls that stand in its way and further enhance Grassy Glide. Rillaboom can also use offensive Tera Types such as Tera Electric, Ground, and Fairy for coverage to hit common checks harder. Some useful defensive Tera types include Fire, Steel, and Poison, which create better defensive matchups and remove specific weaknesses.
