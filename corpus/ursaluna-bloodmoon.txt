Kingambit

Dragonite

is a good partner for Ursaluna-B, as it can switch into any Water- or Grass-type attack from the likes of Dondozo or

Sinistcha

, handle many other attacks with Multiscale, and provide Tailwind support while also using Haze to remove boosts from Dondozo,

Volcarona

Dual screens from

Strategy Comments

Life Orb Ursaluna-B's main role is to be a consistent burst-damage attacker, weakening the opponent's team to clear a pathway for faster teammates or setup sweepers like Sneasler, Volcarona, and Gholdengo. Assault Vest Ursaluna-B is a bulkier attacker that can sit in front of top-tier special attackers such as Gholdengo and Archaludon and easily take them down. Finally, Leftovers Ursaluna-B can stall out the majority of Pokemon with sleep, allowing its teammates to start setting up or dish out attacks, all the while dealing good chip damage.

Life Orb Ursaluna-B should focus on immediately dishing out damage, Assault Vest sets should support its teammates like Rillaboom and Gholdengo by removing problematic Pokemon for them like Incineroar, and Leftovers sets should punish passive play and deny a setup sweeper with Yawn. Ursaluna-B can be brought out mid- or late-game if the opponent's team has Pokemon Ursaluna-B struggles to break through like Porygon2 and Amoonguss, which can be withered by its partners.

Sneasler outspeeds Ursaluna-B, even when Ursaluna-B is under Tailwind if Sneasler's Unburden is activated, and can inflict massive damage with Close Combat. Setting Trick Room can ease the matchup against Sneasler, and redirection from Amoonguss and Indeedee-F can also protect Ursaluna-B from it.

Rain teams that have many Water-types like

Pelipper

and

Basculegion-M

can give Ursaluna-B a hard time, forcing Ursaluna-B to Terastallize. Against these teams, Trick Room or redirection support help a lot because Trick Room helps Ursaluna-B have the Speed advantage against Basculegion-M, and redirection can redirect powerful attacks from Archaludon, Basculegion-M, and Pelipper.

Assault Vest Rillaboom is a good check for Ursaluna-B, as it can threaten huge damage with Wood Hammer and survive Blood Moon. Steel-types and Dragon-types like Gholdengo and Dragonite can resist Rillaboom's Grass-type attacks and retaliate for good damage. Gholdengo can use Nasty Plot to overwhelm Rillaboom with boosted Shadow Ball and Make It Rain, while Dragonite can wall Rillaboom with its defensive typing and chip down Rillaboom with Scale Shot and Extreme Speed. Additionally, Incineroar can weaken Rillaboom with Intimidate and Will-O-Wisp, and it can threaten Rillaboom with Flare Blitz.

The combination of

Maushold

and Annilhilape can spell trouble for Ursaluna-B; Maushold weakens Ursaluna-B's attacks with Friend Guard and can redirect its Blood Moon and Earth Power, while

Annihilape

can repeatedly use Drain Punch. Redirection from supporters like Indeedee-F and

Electabuzz

can redirect Annihilape's Drain Punch and Maushold's Beat Up, stopping Maushold from giving Annihilape Rage Fist boosts. Ursaluna-B's Hyper Voice bypasses Maushold's Follow Me due to being a spread move, allowing it to deal decent damage to both Maushold and Annihilape.

Ursaluna-B is an unusual combination of wallbreaker and physical wall, leveraging its outstanding bulk with Fluffy to check important threats like

Roaring Moon

and

Kingambit

, set up with Calm Mind, and break open defensive cores that usually cannot slow pivot against it with boosted Blood Moon. With Fluffy, it can even tank hits from

Zamazenta

and

Great Tusk

and OHKO back with Blood Moon. Water Absorb is an alternative that lets it block Flip Turn from

Swampert

and

Primarina

, turn

Manaphy

into setup fodder, and check Choice Specs Primordial Sea users, but makes it much less reliable of a physical check. Blood Moon and Earth Power have excellent neutral coverage, letting it run both Calm Mind and Moonlight to set up comfortably on more passive teams. With 13 Speed IVs, it underspeeds level 99

Corviknight

; this turns what would be a reliable pivot into setup fodder, as Corviknight cannot Roost or pivot safely without the switch-in risking a Blood Moon.

Ursaluna-B fits well on balance and offensive teams, as it provides a notable defensive presence while still putting pressure on or holes in opposing teams. Faster special attackers like

Deoxys-S

,

Zapdos

, and

Gholdengo

appreciate Ursaluna-B wearing down and potentially breaking through common shared checks like RegenVest Manaphy. Even physical attackers benefit from it, as Corviknight and Great Tusk will commonly come in on it to recover or attempt to revenge kill it, only to find themselves chipped, KOed, or set up on. Ursaluna-B's special bulk is very poor, even with a Calm Mind up, so solid specially defensive teammates like its own RegenVest Manaphy are highly appreciated. Due to Fluffy's drawbacks, Ursaluna-B is unable to check some common physical attackers, especially the

Ogerpon

formes,

Chien-Pao

, and Pyro Ball

Cinderace

; at least one other reliable secondary physically defensive Pokemon like

Pecharunt

, Corviknight, or Regenerator Primarina is near-mandatory. Ursaluna-B is unable to hit Earth Eater / Levitate Ghost-types like Pecharunt and Gholdengo, so a Dark-type like Roaring Moon or

Ting-Lu

is also recommended.

Other Options

Ursaluna-B can run a number of other immunity abilities like Storm Drain, Well-Baked Body, Bulletproof, and even Sap Sipper if the team needs the immunity, but they all have a much narrower range of setup opportunities compared to Fluffy. To beat Ground-immune Ghost-types, Ursaluna-B can either run Snarl at the expense of Calm Mind or Moonlight or run Mold Breaker or Mind's Eye to still hit them with its main moves. Substitute lets it punish slow pivots without having to predict, but it is generally less useful than its main moves. Offensive wallbreaking sets like Tinted Lens and Adaptability can be run on more offensive teams to provide more immediate power, but they mostly beat the same threats as Fluffy, just more quickly. Lastly, Steam Engine Weakness Policy Ursaluna-B is very funny on dual screens hyper offense, as it outspeeds everything at +6 and OHKOes almost everything at +2, but it is very unreliable.

Draft Order

: Round 4 onwards

Price Range

: 11-12 points

Overview

: Ursaluna-B is a strong Pokemon that offers great setup potential and defensive utility. Its powerful STAB combination, combined with Mind's Eye, is resisted by very few Pokemon, and its physical defenses make it very difficult to break through. However, Ursaluna-B lacks Speed and Special Defense, allowing many special wallbreakers to easily break through it, and it is a Ground-type that lacks entry hazard options, making it less desirable than alternative Ground-types. It is also a very one-dimensional Pokemon that opponents can easily counterprep against.

Common Roles

Setup Sweeper

: With Calm Mind, Ursaluna-B can become a threatening wincon, especially by patching up its weak Special Defense and staying healthy with Moonlight.

Wallbreaker

: Rather than setting up, Ursaluna-B can focus on wallbreaking straight away to break through specific foes, allowing its teammates to shine late-game.

Physical Wall

: Even without defensive investment, Ursaluna-B can eat physical hits from specific opposing Pokemon. Leftovers is good for additional longevity, and Rocky Helmet can also be used to punish pivoting and Knock Off.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Blood Moon, Hyper Voice, Earth Power

Setup Moves

: Calm Mind

Utility Moves

: Moonlight, Roar, Yawn, Substitute

Coverage

: Moonblast, Vacuum Wave, Focus Blast, Snarl, Body Press

Niche Moves

Endure

: Endure can be used along with Custap Berry to safely deal massive damage to a faster Pokemon.

Fake Tears

: Fake Tears can be used on incoming specially defensive walls to make them either take a strong attack or switch out into something else that does not take Ursaluna-B's attacks as well.

Common Items

Leftovers

: Leftovers is great for longevity, since most physical attacks already do very little damage to Ursaluna-B.

Sitrus Berry

: Sitrus Berry is another longevity option, especially when setting up against special attackers with a Calm Mind set.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots is good against teams with solid entry hazard setters, especially if Ursaluna-B wants to switch in and out often.

Resistance Berries

: Resistance Berries, such as Chople Berry, are good for taking one-time hits from offensive foes that threaten Ursaluna-B, allowing it to deal massive damage in return.

Rocky Helmet

: With Ursaluna-B's high Defense, Rocky Helmet is a good option to use against physical threats and pivoters.

Niche Items

Choice Specs

: Choice Specs can allow Ursaluna-B to deal huge amounts of damage to foes, but it forces Ursaluna-B to switch out or use Struggle after using Blood Moon.

Assault Vest

: Assault Vest can patch up Ursaluna-B's low Special Defense while still allowing Ursaluna-B to function as an offensive threat.

Mental Herb

: Mental Herb allows Ursaluna-B to heal or set up without getting disrupted.

Custap Berry

: Custap Berry can be used alongside Endure to quickly outspeed an opposing Pokemon once.

Tera

Ursaluna-B is typically banned from Terastallizing, since better defensive Tera types make it incredibly difficult to take down. If it is allowed to Terastallize, it can use types such as Tera Fairy and Ghost to stop opposing setup sweepers or set up more easily. Offensively, Tera Normal and Ground are great for dealing more damage.

Draft Strategy

Ursaluna-B is good at wallbreaking to enable other wincons, taking attacks from threatening physical wallbreakers, or being a setup sweeper of its own with the right support. However, it has some glaring weaknesses that can be worked around when combined with the right Pokemon.

Pivots

: Ursaluna-B shines offensively when it is able to control opposing momentum by forcing switches. Having solid pivots allows Ursaluna-B to easily switch in against Pokemon that struggle against it and lets it immediately become a threat. Good pivoting options include

Greninja

,

Uxie

, and

Zapdos

Special Walls

: Ursaluna-B is immediately threatened by specially offensive wallbreakers, so it's important to have a Pokemon that doesn't mind taking incoming special attacks, such as

,

Galarian Slowking

, and

Rotom-W

.

Entry Hazard Setters

: Ursaluna-B is a Ground-type that lacks entry hazards, which is pretty uncommon. This means Pokemon of other types will need to handle hazard setting. Otherwise, if another Ground-type is drafted for hazards, the team will gain some glaring weaknesses. Some non-Ground-type hazard setters that pair well with Ursaluna-B include

Glimmora

,

Metagross

, and

Hisuian Samurott

.

Phazing

: Ursaluna-B can be a bit of a sitting duck at times, since it cannot make progress against bulkier setup sweepers. While it has Roar and Yawn, most Pokemon that set up on it are special attackers that make it want to switch out instead. Pokemon that use moves such as Encore and Whirlwind such as

Scream Tail

,

Tinkaton

, and Uxie can help threaten those sweepers and regain momentum.

Checks and Counters

: Ursaluna-B's low Special Defense makes it struggle against special attacking wallbreakers and setup sweepers such as

Chip Damage

: Ursaluna-B is a Pokemon that likes to stay in for long periods of time and struggles with taking passive damage over time. Inflicting it with Toxic and wearing it down with entry hazards or other forms of chip damage make Ursaluna-B struggle more against Pokemon that it would typically wall.

Heal Blocking

: Taunt and Encore can shut Ursaluna-B down hard, especially if it has low health and wants to heal. Additionally, Psychic Noise is a very safe move for opponents to bring against it, since it can deal lots of damage while keeping its health low.
