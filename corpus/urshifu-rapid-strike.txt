Items

Punching Glove

: Punching Gloves protects Urshifu-R from taking devastating damage by hitting Rocky Helmet or Rough Skin with Surging Strikes, in addition to boosting other punching moves. Just note that these moves will now be blocked by Protect and other variants.

Focus Sash

: While it might seem strange with Urshifu-R's good physical bulk, Focus Sash lets it survive extremely powerful physical moves such as Chien-Pao's Electric Tera Blast without investing in bulk, as well as letting it survive powerful special attackers from Flutter Mane, Miraidon, and Calyrex-S.

Draft Order

: Round 3 and beyond

Price Range

: 14-15 points

Overview

: Urshifu-R makes a mark in Draft due to its excellent offensive wallbreaking and sweeping capabilities, especially when made a Tera Captain. With a strong dual typing in addition to a high Attack stat, Urshifu-R can be incredibly difficult to check defensively. This is only made more difficult by the common use of Tera, setup options, and damage boosting items which all allow Urshifu-R to force significant progress each time it switches in and potentially sweep games. Despite these strengths, a mediocre Speed stat, subpar special bulk, and being severely hindered by anti-contact move measures all cause it to not be picked until round 3 or later.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Urshifu-R can use its overwhelming raw power due to an excellent Attack stat and powerful STAB attacks, to weaken or immediately KO a vast majority of the metagame. Due to its mediocre Speed for an offensive Pokemon, Urshifu-R is often at its best when its job is to come in safely and provide immediate damage.

Setup Sweeper

: Setup variations of Urshifu-R use its access to Attack-boosting moves such as Swords Dance and Bulk Up, for easier KOs and can also use Trailblaze to boost its mediocre Speed, making Urshifu-R less easy to revenge kill. With good timing, these sets turn Urshifu-R into an incredibly potent win condition.

Common Items

Protective Pads / Punching Glove

: These items are used to allow Urshifu-R to safely utilize its plethora of contact moves without being punished by Rocky Helmet or abilities such as Static and Flame Body. If Urshifu-R plans on only using punching moves, then the damage boost from Punching Glove is appreciated; otherwise, Protective Pads are optimal.

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf is used for Urshifu-R to overcome its middling Speed stat, allowing it to fire off additional attacks and function more effectively as a fast pivot.

Choice Band

: Choice Band is a commonly used item to further reduce the list of Pokemon that may switch into Urshifu-R while also allowing it to wallbreak with increased ease.

Mystic Water / Black Belt

: This item may be used to hit certain damage thresholds without locking Urshifu-R into one move.

Niche Items

Leftovers

: Leftovers may find use on bulkier setup oriented sets that use moves such as Substitute and Bulk Up, to increase Urshifu-R's longevity.

Lum Berry

: Lum Berry can be used when facing teams that easily spread burn and paralysis, allowing Urshifu-R to maintain effectiveness. It also can be used to set up on a status move with an element of surprise and increased safety.

Resistance Berries

: These Berries will be useful in situations where you anticipate needing Urshifu-R to live a super effective hit to set up. These also pair well with sets that use Acrobatics, as consumption will bring Acrobatics to full power.

Mental Herb

: Mental Herb will be occasionally found on setup sets, allowing Urshifu-R to shrug off Taunt or Encore from a foe in an attempt to prevent it from setting up.

Clear Amulet

: Clear Amulet can be effective when facing a handful of Intimidate Pokemon that resist Surging Strikes.

Tera

Urshifu-R is a great user of Tera and should primarily be drafted to make it a Tera Captain. Urshifu-R is one the premier users to choose same-type Tera in order to enable its wallbreaking capabilities even further. Some common offensive options include using Tera Electric to break bulkier Water-types and avoid paralysis, Tera Ice to break certain Grass- and Flying-types, and Tera Poison or Steel to break certain Fairy-types. On bulkier setup variants, defensive options such as Tera Fairy, Dragon, and Steel, may be used to provide extra turns to set up and sweep.
