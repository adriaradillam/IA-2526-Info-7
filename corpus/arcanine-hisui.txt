Strategy Comments

Hisuian Arcanine's main role on teams is to function as a decently fast burst damage attacker while providing some utility with Intimidate. It works best early-game, weakening and removing threats on the opposing side for its other teammates like Gholdengo, Sneasler, and Dragonite to clean up, but can be sent out late-game where the opposing team has been weakened enough for Hisuian Arcanine to pick off foes with Rock Slide or Extreme Speed. Clear Amulet Hisuian Arcanine is a great tool against balance compositions with Incineroar, Rillaboom, and Dragonite, while Mirror Herb Hisuian Arcanine is incredibly useful for teams that have a hard time handling teams that have common setup sweepers.

Hisuian Arcanine can apply immediate pressure with Intimidate and its STAB moves for its other teammates, threatening common leads such as Rillaboom, Incineroar, Gholdengo, and Volcarona. It can also sweep with Coaching support from Sneasler, and it appreciates Sneasler and Rillaboom being able to weaken or remove both Ursaluna formes,

Basculegion-M

Water- and Ground-types can give Hisuian Arcanine a hard time due to it not having any way to threaten reliable damage against them, especially both Ursaluna formes. Hisuian Arcanine also doesn't appreciate rain compositions, which weaken its Flare Blitz and always use Basculegion-M to revenge kill it and Archaludon to sponge its attacks. Using Hisuian Arcanine with teammates that help against these problems is recommended; Rillaboom can remove and deter the use of Water- and Ground-types with its STAB moves, while Annihilape and Sneasler can reliably threaten Archaludon.

Price Range

Overview

: Hisuian Arcanine is an excellent wallbreaker thanks to its solid Attack stat combined with extremely strong STAB moves and a lack of recoil damage thanks to Rock Head. There are very few Pokemon that want to switch into its strong STAB moves, and it can make up for its lower Speed tier with Extreme Speed. It even has some utility with Intimidate and Stealth Rock and can use Morning Sun to prolong its stay on the battlefield. However, its weakness to Stealth Rock, mediocre bulk, and unimpressive base Speed hold it back from being a stronger threat.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Hisuian Arcanine's extremely powerful STAB moves and respectable Attack mean any Pokemon has to be careful switching in. Its solid Speed means it outspeeds most defensive Pokemon, and Choice Band boosts its Attack to sky-high levels. The combination of Head Smash, Flare Blitz, and Close Combat is unresisted, and Hisuian Arcanine has a number of coverage moves to hit OHKO or 2HKO thresholds.

Defensive Pivot

: Heavy-Duty Boots, Intimidate, and Morning Sun let Hisuian Arcanine play a utility role, coming in on physical threats and forcing them out through Intimidate and the threat of Will-O-Wisp. It can stay healthy with Morning Sun and force progress with Stealth Rock or the threat of a strong Flare Blitz or Head Smash.

Revenge Killer / Cleaner

: Hisuian Arcanine has a great Speed tier for Choice Scarf, and its strong STAB moves can take care of most setup sweepers. It even has Extreme Speed as a priority option in case its foe is still faster.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Head Smash, Flare Blitz, Stone Edge, Raging Fury

Setup Moves

: Agility, Flame Charge

Utility Moves

: Will-O-Wisp, Morning Sun, Roar, Stealth Rock

Coverage

: Extreme Speed, Crunch, Iron Head, Outrage, Psychic Fangs, Wild Charge, Close Combat, Tera Blast

Niche Moves

Rock Tomb / Bulldoze

: Rock Tomb and Bulldoze can help with speed control on more defensive sets and ensure certain foes cannot set up.

Rock Blast

: Rock Blast is more accurate than Head Smash and can bypass Sturdy and Focus Sash, letting Hisuian Arcanine stop certain revenge killers from ruining its day.

Special Attacks

: Hisuian Arcanine has a decent base Special Attack stat, so if your opponent has a good physical check to it that would die to be KOed by a special attack from it, you can slap Choice Specs on it to catch your opponent off guard.

Common Items

Choice Band

: Choice Band boosts Hisuian Arcanine's Attack to absurd levels and, in combination with strong moves like Head Smash and Flare Blitz, lets it threaten 2HKOs or OHKOs on a majority of Pokemon.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots gets rid of Hisuian Arcanine's weakness to Stealth Rock and allows it to switch in more freely, either to continually use strong attacks throughout the game or use Intimidate and threaten Will-O-Wisp against foes with its bulkier sets.

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf lets Hisuian Arcanine act as a powerful revenge killer, outspeeding most unboosted Pokemon while still being a threatening presence.

Niche Items

Other Damage-boosting Items

: Other damage-boosting items such as Charcoal and Life Orb can be used to boost Hisuian Arcanine's damage output without locking it into a move.

Resistance Berries

: If there is a specific threat that only Hisuian Arcanine can check and it can only survive against it with the help of a resistance Berry, then it can be worthwhile to use one.

Rocky Helmet

: Rocky Helmet in combination with Intimidate can punish physical attackers with contact moves and also help punish U-turn.

Protective Pads

: Hisuian Arcanine makes contact with almost all of its offensive moves, so Protective Pads can keep it safe from Rough Skin or Rocky Helmet.

Tera

Hisuian Arcanine doesn't need to be a Tera Captain, as it's threatening even without a boost to its STAB moves from Tera. If you do choose to use it as a Tera Captain, Tera Rock and Fire help boost Hisuian Arcanine's best traits in its incredibly powerful attacks. Tera Fighting lets it have completely unresisted STAB coverage, and Tera Normal gives it strong priority in STAB Extreme Speed. Tera types like Tera Grass, Water, and Flying let it flip its defensive weaknesses into resistances while giving it solid coverage options, while Tera Fairy is a strong defensive type that works well with Hisuian Arcanine's defensive pivot set.

Draft Strategy

Hisuian Arcanine should be drafted when your team is in need of a strong physical wallbreaker. It can muscle past most defensive Pokemon, and it can severely weaken the toughest of defensive checks, allowing your setup sweepers to close out games.

Slow Pivots

: Hisuian Arcanine is not the bulkiest Pokemon, and it has a poor defensive typing. Hard switching it in on an attack leaves it with little health to do its job, and with its offensive sets not running Heavy-Duty Boots, it can be easily chipped down by entry hazards. Pokemon like
