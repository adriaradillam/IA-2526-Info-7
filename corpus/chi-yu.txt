to outspeed Chi-Yu and take advantage of its weak defenses.

Additionally, Wide Guard users like Zamazenta-C and Lunala can take advantage of Chi-Yu locked into Snarl or Heat Wave.

Due to its lack of bulk, priority moves can threaten Chi-Yu severely, namely Aqua Jet from Urshifu-R, Sucker Punch from

Chien-Pao

Strong partners for Chi-Yu include powerful special attackers, such as Calyrex-S and Miraidon, which can take advantage of its Beads of Ruin while threatening Urshifu-R and Koraidon.

Koraidon also pairs very well with Chi-Yu, as not only do they form a powerful core offensively, with Chi-Yu threatening Lunala and Calyrex-S and Koraidon threatening Incineroar and Urshifu-R, but Koraidon also provides sun to bolster Chi-Yu's Fire-type attacks.

Flutter Mane is another common partner for Chi-Yu, as it benefits from Beads of Ruin to bolster its damage output, while Flutter Mane can run Taunt to deny Wide Guard and Trick Room.

Chi-Yu also appreciates redirection support to alleviate its frailty, commonly from Brute Bonnet and Indeedee-F, which can also provide support with Spore and Psychic Terrain, respectively.

Draft Order

: Round 4 onwards

Price Range

: 15-16 points

Overview

: Chi-Yu is the most powerful wallbreaker in the metagame; it is able to cleave its way through almost any foe, including ones that resist its attacks. It tends to be one-dimensional and requires a lot of support from teammates, but the trade-off is incredibly worth it. Its main downsides are its weakness to entry hazards, especially since it usually wants to use Choice items, and its average Speed that can get taken advantage of by many of the metagame's faster threats.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Coupled with Choice Specs, Chi-Yu aims to OHKO or 2HKO every single Pokemon slower than it in a matchup. It tends to only come in a handful of times per game due to its crippling weakness to entry hazards, but it makes the most of these few turns to devastate the opposing team.

Cleaner

: Chi-Yu can utilize its respectable Speed tier along with Choice Scarf to try and win games after its teammates have chipped down the opposing team for either of its STAB moves to win.

Setup Sweeper

: On the rare occasion that it's not strong enough, Chi-Yu can use the switches it forces to set up a Nasty Plot and try to sweep thanks to its powerful STAB moves and access to Flame Charge to boost its Speed.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Overheat, Fire Blast, Flamethrower, Dark Pulse

Setup Moves

: Nasty Plot, Flame Charge

Utility Moves

: Substitute, Taunt, Will-O-Wisp

Coverage

: Hex, Psychic, Tera Blast

Niche Moves

Ruination

: On the off chance that Chi-Yu is unable to 2HKO something outright, it can use Ruination and subsequently Overheat to try and pick it off.

Memento

: This functions similarly to Eject Pack + Overheat, utilizing the immediate pivot and debuff to both of your foes' offensive stats, giving Chi-Yu's teammates an easier chance to set up.

Common Items

Choice Specs

: Choice Specs makes Chi-Yu an even more powerful wallbreaker by turning most 2HKOs into OHKOs and 3HKOs into 2HKOs.

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf can make Chi-Yu an effective cleaner or revenge killer, allowing it to outspeed a lot of naturally faster foes or setup sweepers at +1 Speed.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Usually seen on setup sets, Heavy-Duty Boots negates Chi-Yu's entry hazard weakness and allows it to come in safely more frequently. It offsets its trade in power for longevity and the ability to use Nasty Plot.

Niche Items

Eject Pack

: Eject Pack enables Chi-Yu to use Overheat and safely bring in a teammate to take the hit or have a free chance at setting up. It does lack the power of Choice Specs, the speed of Choice Scarf, and the longevity of Heavy-Duty Boots, but it can be a great enabler for its teammates.

Tera

Chi-Yu is a solid but one-dimensional Tera Captain. It usually uses STAB Tera types to further boost its wallbreaking capabilities, but it can use a defensive Tera type, such as Tera Fairy, Ghost, or Steel, to resist common priority moves or reduce its Stealth Rock weakness. It can occasionally be used to give Chi-Yu coverage such as Tera Blast Grass against bulky Water-types.

Draft Strategy

Chi-Yu is a relatively one-dimensional wallbreaker that offers little to no defensive utility in most games while also needing a lot of support to both get it onto the field. To keep it healthy and position it properly, it should be paired with the roles below.

Entry Hazard Removal

: With its best two sets being Choice items, Chi-Yu is desperate for good hazard removal, with partners such as

Great Tusk

,

Iron Treads

, and

Terapagos

being great at doing this.

Slow Momentum

: Since Chi-Yu doesn't want to switch in directly to a hit, slow momentum is greatly appreciated, and ideally, these momentum picks should also be to take hits reliably. This leads to Pokemon such as

Galarian Slowking

,

Corviknight

, and

Alomomola

all being worthwhile options.

Entry Hazard Setters

: Choice Scarf Chi-Yu can struggle to net certain OHKOs without prior chip damage, and it appreciates hazards to help reach these OHKOs. Hazard setters such as

Hisuian Samurott

,

Ting-Lu

, and

Glimmora

are all worthwhile pairings.

Speed Control

: Due to it only having base 100 Speed, Chi-Yu appreciates being paired with faster threats that can outpace opposing teams. Good examples of this are

Iron Valiant

,

Tornadus-T

, and

Cyclizar

.

Checks and Counters

Entry Hazards

: Since Chi-Yu often relies on Choice items for additional wallbreaking power or Speed, it can quickly get chipped down by entry hazards, especially Stealth Rock.

Faster Pokemon / Priority

: Since it only has base 100 Speed, Chi-Yu is often forced out easily by faster threats that are able to reliably outspeed it and threaten to KO it, including

Greninja

, Iron Valiant, and

Landorus

. Strong priority attackers like

Palafin

,

Dragonite

, and

Conkeldurr

can also easily revenge kill it.

Bulky Assault Vest Users

: While a lot of specially defensive walls are naturally 2HKOed by Choice Specs Chi-Yu, they are able to take extra hits by using Assault Vest and hit it back incredibly hard. Examples of this include

Tentacruel

,

Quaquaval

, and

Raging Bolt

.
