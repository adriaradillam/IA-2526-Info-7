Flamigo's unique offensive typing and access to U-turn to pivot make it stand out from the other Fighting-types in NU. Scrappy allows Flamigo to hit Ghost-types like

Chandelure

and

Hisuian Typhlosion

as well as Tera Ghost users like

Cinccino

and

Porygon-Z

, which would usually block its Close Combat. Choice Scarf allows Flamigo to consistently revenge kill faster threats like

Inteleon

,

Kilowattrel

, and

Tornadus

, while Choice Band gives Flamigo the ability to 2HKO defensive tanks like

Galarian Slowbro

and

Vaporeon

with entry hazard support. However, faster Choice Scarf users, such as

Flygon

and

Munkidori

, threaten and can easily revenge kill Flamigo with their STAB attacks. Additionally, Flamigo’s STAB attacks can be easily played around thanks to it being Choice-locked and its STAB moves inviting common switch-ins like Galarian Slowbro,

Bronzong

, and

Gligar

. Flamigo also faces competition from other Fighting-types like

Toxicroak

,

Paldean Tauros-W

, and

Flamigo easily forces out Steel-, Fighting-, Grass-, and Dark-types like

Registeel

,

Paldean Tauros-W

,

, and

Incineroar

, which grants it free turns to use Roost, allowing it to maintain longevity and use Brave Bird more often. Throat Chop can be run so that Flamigo can better threaten

Swords Dance Flamigo aims to capitalize on passive foes such as

Wo-Chien

and

Swampert

On balanced teams, Flamigo uses Roost so that it can repeatedly come in regardless of Stealth Rock being up and take advantage of the aforementioned walls, not worrying about their Leech Seed and Knock Off. In contrast, Flamigo runs Agility on hyper offense teams so that it can run through faster teams, outspeeding even Choice Scarf users such as

Flygon

Munkidori

Not running an item allows Flamigo to use the drawback-free Acrobatics over the health-wasting Brave Bird, whereas Sitrus Berry eases setup for Flamigo on hyper offense builds while allowing it to still use Acrobatics later. The second EV spread is for Flamigo on hyper offensive teams, as it maximizes its Attack and, after an Agility, allows it to outspeed Speed Booster Energy

Other Options

Swords Dance Flamigo can use Protective Pads to avoid adverse contact effects such as paralysis from

Strategy Comments

Flamigo's main role is to be a wallbreaker that can threaten top Pokemon like Gholdengo and Incineroar. Flamigo really shines when against Steel-types like Gholdengo and

Kingambit

, threatening them with Close Combat.

Flamigo is meant to be brought first, but it can be saved in certain matchups, such as the Psychic-type spam matchup, since Wide Guard is able to deny Expanding Force and Eruption from foes such as

Indeedee-M

and

Hisuian Typhlosion

, respectively.

Faster Pokemon like

Dragapult

are some of the biggest threats to Flamigo, as they can bring Flamigo down to Focus Sash or double target Flamigo and KO it. Physically bulky Pokemon such as

Dondozo

can also be an issue, as they can take hits and deal good damage back. Bulky Flying-types like Dragonite and

Corviknight

can take on attacks from Flamigo while hitting hard back. Powerful special attackers like Gholdengo can take down the physically bulky foes. Electric- and Ice-type attacks like Thunderbolt and Ice Beam from Porygon2 can threaten these Flying-types, while Clear Smog Amoonguss does well into Dondozo.

Draft Order

: Round 7 onwards

Price Range

: 3-4 points

Overview

: Flamigo is an attacker that can spam its Fighting-type moves even in the face of Ghost-types thanks to its ability Scrappy. Despite a great ability and two great spammable STAB options, its Speed stat is lackluster, its bulk is near unusable, and without items to significantly boost its power, it is not strong enough to make up for any of it.

Common Roles

Revenge Killer

: By holding a Choice Scarf, Flamigo can act as a revenge killer that can also use U-turn to generate offensive momentum for its team.

Wallbreaker

: Having the option to hold a Choice Band or set up with Swords Dance, Flamigo can serve as a wallbreaker that can spam its Fighting-type move worry-free thanks to Scrappy.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Close Combat, Brave Bird, Low Kick

Setup Moves

: Bulk Up, Swords Dance, Agility

Utility Moves

: Roost, Taunt, U-turn

Coverage

: Liquidation, Lunge, Throat Chop

Niche Moves

Sky Attack / Acrobatics

: While it is less reliable than Brave Bird and relies on a Power Herb, Sky Attack could be used alongside Acrobatics if a stronger Flying-type move is needed. With an easily consumable item such as Power Herb, Acrobatics is about as powerful as Brave Bird and avoids the recoil damage.

Upper Hand

: Flamigo is easily KOed with priority moves, meaning it can use Upper Hand to try and gain the upper hand against foes that want to do this.

Tailwind

: Tailwind can be used to speed itself up without having to hold a Choice Scarf, and U-turn can be used alongside it to allow Flamigo to pass that Speed boost to its teammates.

Common Items

Choice Scarf

: Flamigo needs to outspeed its foes in order to gain any value, and Choice Scarf ensures that most unboosted Pokemon will be outsped.

Choice Band

: If Flamigo wants more punching power, Choice Band can be used to break through the defensive Pokemon that would otherwise want to switch into it.

Niche Items

Damage-boosting Items

: Items like Life Orb and Black Belt can give Flamigo the power it needs to break through some Pokemon while still being able to switch its moves.

Power Herb

: With Sky Attack, Power Herb can provide a one-time powerful attack that does not have the recoil damage like Brave Bird does.

Lum Berry

: By allowing Flamigo to set up against Pokemon that want to debilitate it with a status effect, Lum Berry can be used to transform it into a strong sweeper.

Tera

Flamigo is too frail, weak, and slow to take advantage of Terastallization. Its offensive typing has good synergy, with both combining to form a near unresisted combination. If it is made a Tera Captain, Tera Fighting and Flying are the only two types that make sense, giving a boost to whichever STAB option is better in the matchup.

Draft Strategy

Flamigo likes to be on bulky offense teams that have allies it can pivot to with U-turn but also take advantage of its role as a revenge killer. Pokemon that it can form pivot cores with are also helpful, allowing the offensive pressure it gives to span beyond its own reaches.

Offensive Pivots

: Pokemon like

Greninja

,

Meowscarada

, and

Roaring Moon

that do not want to switch in directly but can continue to gather momentum through their own U-turns can increase Flamigo’s worth.

Defensive Pivot

: Pokemon like

Iron Treads

,

Landorus-T

, and

Rotom-W

that can tank hits and get other teammates in make Flamigo much easier to use its strong moves without having to worry about locking itself into something that could be punished.

Checks and Counters

Faster Pokemon

: Faster Pokemon such as

Iron Valiant

,

Enamorus

, and

Zarude

that commonly have ways to boost their Speed will outspeed and easily KO Flamigo.

Physically Defensive Walls

: Pokemon like

Pecharunt

,

Uxie

, and

Corviknight

that can shrug off Flamigo’s attacks are common answers to it.

Chip Damage / Recoil

: Damage taken from Brave Bird's recoil and Rocky Helmet adds up, allowing Pokemon like

Palafin

Scizor

Fairy-type Pokemon

: Although Fairy-type Pokemon often have mediocre physical bulk, they have no problem switching into Flamigo’s Fighting-type moves and threatening it out with their own Fairy-type moves.
