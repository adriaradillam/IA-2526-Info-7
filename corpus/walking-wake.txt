Overview

: Walking Wake is a premier threat on sun teams and a somewhat fast special attacker with strong STAB options in Hydro Steam and Draco Meteor. These traits can be boosted further by activating Protosynthesis with sun-setting teammates or Booster Energy. However, Walking Wake's shallow movepool, substantial price, and one-dimensional playstyle, often necessitating sun to unlock its fullest potential, lead to difficulty fitting it onto most rosters in favor of more flexible options.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Walking Wake boasts fantastic raw power in the sun and can use both Hydro Steam and Draco Meteor alongside damage-boosting items to greatly dent opposing teams and force KOs.

Late-game Cleaner

: Walking Wake can use Speed Booster Energy or Agility in tandem with Special Attack Booster Energy to become faster than the vast majority of Pokemon. By utilizing its strong STAB combination and offensive stats, especially alongside sun setters like

Ninetales

and

Torkoal

, Walking Wake can pick up KOs on weakened foes or clean up against the opposing team.

Offensive Utility

: Walking Wake has access to some great utility options in Flip Turn, Knock Off, and Scald. These options allow it to support its team by maintaining some offensive prowess even when faced with Pokemon that can wall it.

Common Moves

Primary STAB moves

Setup moves

: Agility

Coverage

: Flamethrower, Weather Ball, Hurricane

Niche Moves

Dragon Dance / Physical Moves

: Walking Wake can use Dragon Dance to surprise checks that are anticipating strong special attacks, adequately boosting its physical STAB options and coverage to wallbreak or clean games.

Sunny Day

: With Sunny Day, Walking Wake can set sun in emergencies to activate its own Protosynthesis and attempt to clean.

Aqua Jet

: Despite Walking Wake's relatively low Attack, Aqua Jet can be useful in providing emergency speed control for scenarios where an opposing Pokemon is weakened.

Roar

: Roar can be used as an emergency option to phaze boosting sweepers.

Common Items

Choice Specs

: Choice Specs is Walking Wake's strongest and most consistent form of boosting its damage output, working extremely well with a Protosynthesis Speed boost.

Booster Energy

: Booster Energy is a great option on late-game cleaner sets, enabling it to cleave through the remnants of the opposing team.

Life Orb

: Life Orb provides Walking Wake less damage output than Choice Specs would but grants it the benefit to change up moves.

Niche Items

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf allows Walking Wake to outspeed most Pokemon, making it a good offensive utility move user or a revenge killer, and also has some use on a late-game cleaner set. However, Walking Wake can use Protosynthesis or Booster Energy to gain Speed for a similar effect and still be able to swap moves.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots enables Walking Wake to hit the field much more often, which is especially useful against entry hazards. It also helps pivot and utility sets proportionally more.

Eject Pack

: With Eject Pack, Draco Meteor can be used on offensive utility sets as a nuclear one-time pivot option in certain matchups.

Other Damage-boosting Items

: Items that increase overall damage, such as Mystic Water, Dragon Fang, and Expert Belt can be used as an alternative to Choice Specs on wallbreaking sets that want the freedom to swap moves.

Leftovers

: Leftovers gives Walking Wake slightly more longevity and is a good fit on utility or Substitute sets.

Assault Vest

: Assault Vest increases Walking Wake's special bulk while allowing it to maintain some offensive presence and is therefore a decent fit on offensive utility sets.

Resistance Berries

: One-time resistance Berries can be used to let Walking Wake take a super effective attack from a strong foe and knock it out in return or set up with Agility.

Tera

Walking Wake is an effective Tera Captain on sun teams, being able to boost both STAB options to ludicrous levels of damage if allowed to use Tera Water or Dragon. Terastallization can also sometimes be used to hit a few of Walking Wake's checks with Tera Blast or to gain some survivability via defensive types like Steel and Fairy.
