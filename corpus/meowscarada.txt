Draft Order

: Early round 1 onwards

Price Range

: 19-20 points

Overview

: Meowscarada is a great versatile pick that is an excellent enabler for a draft's centerpiece or wincons. With Protean, it serves as an excellent wallbreaker, revenge killer, or fast pivot with its great coverage options. It is great at forcing opposing switches, allowing it to maintain momentum with STAB U-turn. However, it's relatively frail and struggles to take hits, so it must be brought in safely.

Common Roles

Revenge Killer

: Meowscarada can run a Choice Scarf to outspeed other Choice Scarf users, making it an incredibly powerful revenge killer that can maintain momentum with U-turn.

Fast Pivot

: Meowscarada's high Speed allows it to outspeed opposing threats and force them out with its powerful attacks. It can also use STAB U-turn with Protean to do lots of damage to switch-ins and position teammates well. When paired with Choice Band, Meowscarada becomes unwallable in many scenarios, though it becomes prone to being revenge killed.

Lead Entry Hazard Setter

: Meowscarada can be used to set Spikes or Toxic Spikes at the start of the game. This set is often paired with Taunt, attacking moves such as Knock Off and U-turn, and a Focus Sash.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Flower Trick, Knock Off, Sucker Punch

Utility Moves

: Taunt, Spikes, Toxic Spikes, Substitute, Trick, Brick Break

Coverage

: U-turn, Triple Axel, Thunder Punch, Low Kick, Play Rough

Niche Moves

Leaf Storm

: Leaf Storm is good against defensive walls that Meowscarada struggles breaking through. It is most often ran on entry hazard setting or support sets, especially against physically bulky hazard removers like

Great Tusk

.

Leech Seed

: Leech Seed can be used along with fast Substitute to chip down some of Meowscarada's incoming checks, allowing it to regain most of its health while weakening the foe. However, simply pivoting to a teammate that can better check these foes is almost always preferred.

Common Items

Choice Scarf

: Even though Meowscarada is naturally very fast, Choice Scarf is useful for outspeeding Speed-boosting and even faster Pokemon, making it a great revenge killer.

Choice Band

: Choice Band gives Meowscarada great wallbreaking or late-game sweeping potential, especially with its already-strong STAB attacks.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Since Meowscarada likes to pivot in and out frequently, Heavy-Duty Boots is a great item on it, allowing it to avoud entry hazard chip over time.

Niche Items

Focus Sash

: Focus Sash is good for lead sets, allowing them to get extra entry hazards up or deal extra damage.

Damage-boosting Items

: Other damage-boosting items are useful for providing additional damage output, especially when getting Choice-locked into a single move is unideal. It may be better to run Overgrow instead of Protean on these sets to maintain its STAB types.

Protective Pads

: Even though Flower Trick does not make contact, Protective Pads is good against Pokemon with contact-related items and abilities, especially when using Triple Axel. The main downside is not being able to utilize other items, especially Heavy-Duty Boots. They both aim to reduce chip damage, so keep in mind which item is more valuable in a given matchup.

Choice Specs

: Meowscarada has a decent special movepool, so Choice Specs can be used in matchups where its strong special STAB moves and coverage are enough to offset its lower Special Attack stat.

Leftovers

: Leftovers is good for passive recovery, especially on Substitute + Leech Seed builds.

Tera

Meowscarada does not benefit from Terastallization, since it completely negates Protean. If it is made a Tera Captain regardless, it can utilize Tera Grass and Dark to increase STAB damage or Tera Fire for extra coverage.

Draft Strategy

Meowscarada is a great versatile Pokemon that synergizes very well with another top-tier Pokemon. It provides valuable chip damage, item removal, and pivoting, and it can occasionally clean up teams that were weakened by its teammates.

High-tier Tera Captains

: Since Meowscarada isn't reliant on Terastallization to function, it pairs extremely well with many of the top-tier Tera Captains in the metagame, including

Raging Bolt

,

Enamorus

, and

Salamence

Special Wallbreakers

: Meowscarada can be an excellent physical wallbreaker, but it often struggles breaking physical walls. Having a special wallbreaker that it can pivot to keeps its team in a strong position, and it forces the opponent to choose what form of defensive EVs they want to run on their Pokemon. Some good pairings include

Iron Valiant

,

Terapagos

, and

Iron Bundle

.

Ground-types

: Ground-types like Great Tusk and

Landorus

can help check the Poison-, Steel-, and Fire-types that may otherwise wall Meowscarada. Since it can force many switches, it can easily pivot to them safely.

Checks and Counters

Poison-types

: Many Poison-types can check Meowscarada well, especially if they are paired with Rocky Helmet to punish it from using U-turn.

Muk

and

Swalot

cannot get their Rocky Helmet removed, and they are often drafted on teams that struggle against Meowscarada.

Other Physically Defensive Walls

: Few Pokemon can comfortably take Meowscarada's hits, but physically bulky Pokemon that can resist its attacks are typically most reliable, especially if they force Choice sets to make difficult predictions. Examples include

Corviknight

,

Gouging Fire

, and

Galarian Weezing

U-turn

: Any faster Pokemon with U-turn can easily force Meowscarada out due to its massive weakness to the move.

Chip Damage

: Since Meowscarada uses U-turn a lot, Rocky Helmet and other forms of contact damage such as Rough Skin can quickly chip it down. Damage from entry hazards also adds up quickly, especially if it does not want to run Heavy-Duty Boots.

Priority

: Meowscarada struggles to take priority attacks, especially after being chipped from entry hazards or contact damage. Vacuum Wave and Mach Punch are very threatening, and moves like Bullet Punch and Extreme Speed can also do massive damage.
