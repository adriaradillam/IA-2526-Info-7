Wave Crash is a very strong STAB move. With Stealth Rock support and Tera Water's power boost, it crushes walls Palafin normally might struggle to break, like

Toxapex

and

Corviknight

, and always OHKOes

Miraidon

.

Close Combat is a strong, reliable coverage move that hits many of Palafin's checks like Corviknight and

Koraidon

for solid damage, while also hitting Water-types barring Toxapex for neutral damage.

Ice Punch allows Palafin to hit Dragon-types like the very common Koraidon and Miraidon while also hitting Water Absorb

Clodsire

. However, it cannot OHKO Koraidon and is not guaranteed to OHKO Miraidon.

Zen Headbutt allows Palafin to hit Toxapex super effectively, as well as potentially OHKOing Koraidon and having fantastic odds to 2HKO Miraidon.

Tera Water powers up Palafin's Water-type moves and allows Palafin to achieve feats like 2HKOing Miraidon with Jet Punch from full HP if rain is up.

Choice Band Palafin functions best on offensive teams to revenge kill threats like

Flutter Mane

or to break walls for its teammates mid-game. Remember, base Palafin needs to be used very early in a game to transform into its Hero Form.

Pelipper

is a great partner for Palafin, as it checks Koraidon decently and replaces its sun with rain, strengthening Palafin's powerful Water-type attacks.

Koraidon sets up sun and resists Water-type attacks Palafin throws at it, so Palafin appreciates teammates that threaten Koraidon, like

Iron Bundle

and Flutter Mane. Also, Palafin is weakened by the sun Koraidon sets up, so it is best not to pair Palafin and Koraidon together.

Miraidon is a good partner, as it destroys

Alomomola

, a great counter to Palafin, and can safely bring Palafin onto the field with either U-turn or Volt Switch. Miraidon also appreciates Palafin threatening Ground-type Pokemon that can take Miraidon's Electric-type attacks, like

Ting-Lu

,

Iron Treads

, and Clodsire.

Jet Punch's strong priority allows this Palafin set to still be threatening to offensive builds, while Drain Punch keeps Palafin healthy against bulkier teams.

Taunt allows Palafin to prevent common responses to setup sweepers from working, such as Whirlwind from

Ting-Lu

and Haze from

Toxapex

, while also preventing Pokemon like

Annihilape

from setting up alongside it.

Fairy is the Tera type of choice to allow Palafin to resist both of

Koraidon

's STAB attacks and potentially set up on Choice Scarf variants of it. Fairy also removes Palafin's weakness to

Iron Bundle

's Freeze-Dry and

Miraidon

's Electric-type moves.

Palafin struggles to deal with Koraidon or Miraidon, which can come in on this set easily, especially given that Zero to Hero forces Palafin to switch at least once before it can be offensively threatening. As a result, this Palafin needs teammates that can defensively handle these threats. Ting-Lu and

Iron Treads

both help with Miraidon, and Pokemon like

Great Tusk

,

Scream Tail

, and

Skeledirge

can provide defensive solidity against Koraidon.

This Palafin set functions best with a sturdy defensive backbone alongside it, to mitigate the impact of Zero to Hero. As a result, it does not work as well on more hyper offensive playstyles.

Bulk Up Palafin builds upon its natural bulk and Attack, letting it function as a strong priority attacker who uses its Defense to brazenly set up and become a threatening sweeper that is difficult to KO for common physical attackers like

Chien-Pao

,

Landorus-T

, and

Kingambit

.
Jet Punch circumvents common speed control and is threatening against the frailer metagame, notably OHKOing

Chi-Yu

and

Landorus

at +1.
Drain Punch is a reliable means of recovery in addition to Leftovers while also hitting popular Dark-types like Kingambit and Chien-Pao, as well as common Water-resistant or immune foes such as

Rillaboom

and

Volcanion

harder.
Tera Grass provides an immunity to

Amoonguss

's Spore and Rage Powder, as well as resistances to Grass- and Electric-type attacks such as Rillaboom's Wood Hammer and

Iron Hands

's Wild Charge, which would otherwise OHKO Palafin.
Palafin's base forme has poor damage output and bulk, so it is often ideal for it to lead and pivot out immediately to activate Zero to Hero. This is so crucial to Palafin's functioning that it is very telegraphed and easily exploitable with powerful attacks directed at it. To cover for this, Palafin may lead together with a Fake Out user or a redirector to reduce incoming damage.
Bulk Up Palafin pairs well with partners that can remove Psychic Terrain, use Fake Out to generate free turns for Palafin to set up, and extend Palafin's longevity. Rillaboom fulfills all of these roles, with Grassy Terrain granting additional passive recovery.

Cresselia

is another good partner, as its Lunar Blessing heals Palafin and cures burn and poison, which would otherwise severely limit Palafin's offensive output or the turns it can stay on field. Amoonguss's Rage Powder redirects threatening attacks such as Iron Hands's Wild Charge, removes Toxic Spikes that reduce Palafin's longevity, and provides a major source of recovery for Palafin with Pollen Puff. Iron Hands is a Fake Out user whose Electric-type attacks eliminate opposing Water-types that resist Jet Punch.

Grimmsnarl

can also increase Palafin's bulk and create setup opportunities with its dual screens. As Amoonguss effortlessly walls Palafin and undoes its progress with Clear Smog, Palafin requires partners that deal with it effectively, like

Heatran

, Chien-Pao, and Chi-Yu.

Draft Order

: Round 2 onwards

Price Range

: 15-16 points

Overview

: Palafin's high Attack lets it puts enormous pressure on opposing teams, especially with Jet Punch to pick off faster foes and Bulk Up to become an effective setup sweeper. Its biggest drawback is its need to use a turn to activate its Hero forme, but it can still be threatening to weakened or Water-weak foes regardless. While the prevalence of faster Grass-types in the metagame can also hold Palafin back, its true limiter is the creativity of its user.

Common Roles

Physical Wallbreaker

: Palafin can tear up teams with Choice Band-boosted STAB moves coming off its extraordinary Attack, even against team compositions with multiple Water-resistant foes. It also has an exceptional ability to clean with access to its strong priority in Jet Punch, and it has plenty of strong coverage moves to hit potential checks.

Setup Sweeper

: With Bulk Up, Jet Punch, great natural bulk, and the ability to heal itself with Drain Punch, Palafin makes for a potent setup sweeper that can instantly end games given the smallest of opportunities.

Special Wallbreaker

: With a respectable base Special Attack and access to coverage like Boomburst, Ice Beam and Grass Knot, Palafin's special sets can be surprisingly potent. Boomburst is often used with Throat Spray, though Life Orb and Choice Specs also have merit. This can take up a cleaner role with Agility in its moveset. Note that Palafin can still fit Jet Punch in special sets, though it would have to take a hit in bulk from using a Defense- or Special Defense-lowering nature.

Common Moves

STAB Moves

: Jet Punch, Wave Crash, Flip Turn, Liquidation, Surf, Hydro Pump

Setup Moves

: Bulk Up, Agility

Utility Moves

: Substitute, Encore, Taunt, Haze

Coverage

: Ice Punch, Drain Punch, Close Combat, Acrobatics, Iron Head, Zen Headbutt, Throat Chop, Body Slam, Ice Beam, Grass Knot, Boomburst, Aura Sphere, Focus Blast

Niche Moves

Fling

: Palafin holding a Big Nugget has access to a one-time 130-Base Power Dark-type move to hit foes like

Slowking

. This also lets it use Acrobatics afterwards.

Facade

: Facade is one of the more niche ways that Palafin has to play around status, it is prone to being weakened by status effects like burn and paralysis.

Common Items

Punching Glove / Protective Pads

: With one of the foremost checks to Palafin being Rocky Helmet, sets with Drain Punch and Jet Punch can use Punching Glove to great effect. Protective Pads replaces this when the set has moves like Flip Turn and Wave Crash.

Sitrus Berry / Leftovers

: Sitrus Berry and Leftovers are not interchangeable, though they have a similar role. Both generally work well on Bulk Up sets, with Sitrus Berry finding itself on sets that either don't intend to last as long or need Acrobatics to hit bulky Grass-types. Leftovers is more useful when the game plan is to use Bulk Up multiple times and sit on the field for an extended duration versus a more passive team.

Damage-boosting Items

: Palafin can makes use of the damage boost from Choice Band really well, especially since it doesn't have to lock itself into a move long-term by using Flip Turn. It can also use items like Mystic Water, Life Orb, and Expert Belt to hit damage thresholds without being locked into a move.

Resistance Berries

: Resistance Berries help lure threats so that Palafin can revenge kill the foe or get an additional turn to set up. This works especially well with Acrobatics against faster Grass-type foes.

Niche Items

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Specifically relevant with Flip Turn, Heavy-Duty Boots lets Palafin safely pivot in and out of the field through avoiding accumulated entry hazard damage.

Assault Vest

: Assault Vest works well on Palafin, as it often runs four attacking moves regardless. This allows it to check and revenge kill specific specially offensive foes that would otherwise OHKO it or deal massive amounts of damage.

Weakness Policy

: Weakness Policy works well with Palafin's solid natural bulk, allowing it to activate off a weak Volt Switch or coverage move that can be expected in prep.

Throat Spray / Choice Specs

: Palafin can use Boomburst to trigger Throat Spray, making it a surprise special wallbreaker. Alternatively, Choice Specs can be used if immediate power is needed and if there is not a need to switch moves.

Choice Scarf

: Choice Scarf is rare, since Jet Punch generally works well enough for speed control, but in some matchups, attacking faster with Wave Crash or coverage moves can be valuable. Jet Punch still works well to hit opposing Choice Scarf users.

Tera

Palafin is always banned from Terastallizing. If yours has the liberty to Terastallize, congratulations in advance. It can run Tera Water for even stronger STAB moves, Tera Fighting to boost damage from Drain Punch and Close Combat, Tera Normal for a stronger Boomburst, Tera Grass or Electric to hit bulky Water-types with Tera Blast, or a defensive option like Tera Fairy or Poison to facilitate setting up.

Draft Strategy

While Palafin can be built to beat many of its checks, it cannot take on all of them at once, and it especially struggles against bulky Water-types and fast Grass-types. Foes must often use specific checks and counters to beat Palafin, so drafting teammates that can take advantage of this is extremely advantageous.

Water-type Checks

: Against Water-types that check Palafin, it can pivot to Grass-types like

Meowscarada

and

Hydrapple

to maintain momentum and chip them down into range of Jet Punch. Many Tera Captains also need Tera Water to check Palafin, so these teammates are especially beneficial.

Top-tier Tera Captains

: Palafin is always banned from Terastallizing, but it synergizes well and creates powerful offensive cores with Tera Captains like

Raging Bolt

,

Zarude

, and

Tornadus-T

.

Entry Hazard Removal

: Reliable hazard removal from Pokemon like

Iron Treads

and

Cyclizar

is generally advised on Palafin drafts, as it dislikes Toxic Spikes and chip damage and seldom wants to run Heavy-Duty Boots.

Bulky Backbones

: Since Palafin needs to swap out of its Zero forme, it greatly appreciates a backbone that it can fall back on before it gets back on the field to take control. Pokemon such as

Ting-Lu

,

Landorus-T

, and

Tinkaton

go a long way to support a Palafin draft with bulk, entry hazards, and additional utility.

Checks and Counters

Water-resistant Pokemon

: Many Water-resistant Pokemon go a long way in keeping Palafin at bay, as it typically wants to spam STAB moves whenever possible. Pokemon such as

Rotom-Wash

,

Slowbro

, and Raging Bolt do a solid job of limiting the sets it can afford to run, while faster Pokemon that resist Jet Punch, like Meowscarada,

Ogerpon-W

, and

Iron Bundle

, provide effective offensive checks, though they should be careful when switching into it directly. Many Tera Captains can also easily run Tera Water to check it.

Residual Damage / Status

: Palafin strongly dislikes taking residual damage from entry hazards and Rocky Helmet, as it has no reliable means of recovery. It also gets strongly crippled by status effects due to additional chip damage as well as key stat drops from paralysis and burn.

Aggressive Play

: Since Palafin is significantly weaker before it has activated its Hero forme, foes can attempt to deny it from doing so, as it is much easier to handle in its base forme. Teams with a strong offensive profile can often exploit the sorts of builds Palafin typically finds itself on, denying it the turns it needs in order to truly become effective.
