Set Details

Galarian Slowking provides a unique function in the metagame by being the best anti-weather option. Its incredible special bulk, decent defensive typing, and access to Regenerator also allow it to function as a very effective slow and bulky pivot. The combination of Future Sight and Chilly Reception allows it to not just safely bring in a teammate but also hammer them with a delayed attack, leaving the foe in an uncomfortable position. Other Options

A set utilizing an Assault Vest maximizes Galarian Slowking's special bulk and allows it to run more coverage options such as Flamethrower and Ice Beam, but Chilly Reception has far too many applications to pass up, and the lack of Heavy-Duty Boots makes Galarian Slowking far more susceptible to being worn down by hazards. All of the aforementioned attacks can be run over Scald or Thunder Wave to catch foes off guard, but the utility the latter two provide is usually preferred. Overview

: Galarian Slowking is a great defensive pivot that enables the offensive threats on your draft. The combination of Galarian Slowking's great typing, good bulk, and Regenerator allow it to tailor its sets to check a large variety of offensive Pokemon. Its respectable base 110 Special Attack and wide array of coverage moves allow it to threaten said offensive Pokemon; however, the prevalence of strong Dark- and Ground-types in the current metagame means that it's unlikely an opponent's draft will have no ways of breaking past Galarian Slowking.

Common Roles

Bulky Pivot

: Galarian Slowking's good mixed bulk and defensive typing allow it to check strong special wallbreakers while threatening them with its STAB moves or coverage options. The combination of Future Sight and Chilly Reception also allows Galarian Slowking to pivot into strong wallbreakers and pressure their checks while staying healthy thanks to Regenerator.

Setup Wallbreaker

: Galarian Slowking can utilize Nasty Plot or Calm Mind to act as a strong wallbreaker against more defensively-oriented teams using its STAB moves and coverage options. Galarian Slowking can also use Iron Defense to survive hits from more offensive teams and strike back with its naturally good Special Attack.

Common Items

: Entry hazards limit Galarian Slowking's ability to consistently check opposing offensive pokemon, so Heavy-Duty Boots are a great way to avoid them and maximize Regenerator recovery while pivoting.

: Galarian Slowking can utilize Black Sludge on setup sets where it does not expect to switch many times throughout the match, or on Trick sets to cripple opposing Pokemon. If Galarian Slowking is a Tera user, Leftovers is the preferred item.

Tera

Galarian Slowking normally should not be one of your Tera captains, as its natural typing is what enables it to check a wide array of offensive threats. If Galarian Slowking is one of your Tera captains, your main choices for a Tera type are those that cover its main weaknesses, such as Dark to check opposing Ghost- and Dark-types or Grass to check opposing Ground-types. Galarian Slowking already has a wide array of coverage moves and thus is unlikely to require Tera Blast for specific coverage, although it may Tera offensively for the power boost on setup sets.
