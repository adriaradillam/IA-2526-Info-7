Deoxys-S outruns and outguns all of the opposition with its blazing base Speed stat alongside its colorful coverage and utility options, giving it great versatility as either a wallbreaker, late-game cleaner, or lead. Checks and Counters

Hatterene

: Hatterene's Magic Bounce ability prevents Deoxys-S from setting up entry hazards while also resisting Psycho Boost. It can also hold an Ability Shield to block Skill Swap.

Grimmsnarl

: Grimmsnarl can use Taunt before Deoxys-S due to Prankster and is immune to Psycho Boost. It does need to be wary of Knock Off removing Light Clay, however, as it puts Reflect and Light Screen on a tighter timer.

Rapid Spin Users

: Rapid Spin users like Iron Treads and Terapagos are able to comfortably tank Deoxys-S's attacks and clear hazards while setting up their own Stealth Rock.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots prevents the holder from taking damage from hazards, effectively rendering Deoxys-S useless into full Heavy-Duty Boots stall teams.

Overview

: Deoxys-S is one of the fastest Pokemon in the format, providing elite speed control as well as executing a range of roles at a high level. It serves as an incredible lead due to its access to Stealth Rock, Spikes, dual screens, and Taunt, which paired with unmatched Speed allow it to kick off games fantastically. Deoxys-S can also be a fantastic offensive piece, as it has a variety of effective setup options and a diverse mixed attacking movepool. With its ability to heavily invest in bulk, it can often survive neutral hits to set up the required boosts to clean a team or wallbreak mid-game. Due to this flexibility and the value of Speed, Deoxys-S finds itself at the top of draft boards, only held back by its lack of bulk and weaker unboosted attacking stats.

Common Roles

Setup Sweeper

: Deoxys-S can boost its Special Attack with Nasty Plot to add strength to its elite Speed and become a powerful threat. Due to its wide range of special coverage, ability to run bulky sets due to its natural Speed, and strong STAB options, a well-supported Deoxys-S can set up with relative ease and is often able to hit an entire opposing team powerfully while outspeeding any unboosted Pokemon or +1 Speed Pokemon below base 104 Speed.

Dedicated Lead

: Deoxys-S is an elite lead Pokemon, as its Speed paired with a Focus Sash allows it to set up entry hazards with ease and potentially shut down an opposing lead with Taunt. It can also perform a similar role with Light Clay, as it has access to dual screens.

All-Out Attacker

: Deoxys-S can easily afford to run dual-invested mixed attacking sets with damage-boosting items, as it rarely requires investment into its Speed stat. These sets typically run four attacks and leverage its wealth of coverage to strongly hit entire teams.

Common Moves

Primary STAB Moves

: Psycho Boost, Psychic, Psyshock, Psychic Noise, Zen Headbutt

Setup Moves

: Nasty Plot, Meteor Beam, Calm Mind, Iron Defense, Cosmic Power

Niche Moves

Stored Power

: Stored Power is used on setup sets, often defensive dual dance ones with Iron Defense and Calm Mind, taking advantage of teams with a poor Dark-type or lack of one and sweeping after some boosts. These sets can pair well with a Weakness Policy to stomach a super effective hit and gain immediate power.

Mirror Coat / Counter

: Mirror Coat and Counter can be found on lead or Focus Sash sets to trade with a foe.

Extreme Speed

: Although Deoxys-S is incredibly fast, Extreme Speed can be a means of avoiding being revenge killed by opposing priority moves on all-out attacker sets. It is particularly useful against Sucker Punch and Thunderclap, as Extreme Speed goes first and makes these attacks fail.

Future Sight

: Future Sight can be used to put pressure on opposing teams and allow Deoxys-S's teammates to find themselves in favorable positions.

Common Items

Damage-boosting Items

: As Deoxys-S is relatively weak, damage-boosting items such as Life Orb and Expert Belt allow it to hit out-of-reach damage thresholds on setup or all-out attacking variants.

Focus Sash

: Focus Sash pairs excellently with dedicated lead sets and setup sets to ignore Deoxys-S's weak natural bulk and give it an extra turn to either set entry hazards or boost its stats.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots allows Deoxys-S to gain some extra longevity by avoiding entry hazard chip damage, especially since it typically wants other moves over Recover.

Niche Items

Eject Pack

: Paired with Psycho Boost and less frequently Superpower, Eject Pack can be used as a way to dish out a powerful attack and maintain momentum.

Power Herb

: Power Herb in conjunction with Meteor Beam can provide Deoxys-S with an effective and powerful means of setup as well as strong Rock-type coverage.

Resistance Berries

: Resistance Berries can be useful to make up for the Deoxys-S's weaknesses and allow it an extra turn to set up, use its utility moves, or lure and trade with a foe.

Light Clay

: Light Clay is useful on lead sets that wish to set dual screens to extend their duration and support their team more effectively.

Sitrus Berry

: To avoid being KOed by certain attacks, Sitrus Berry can occasionally prove useful for increasing Deoxys-S's longevity.

Weakness Policy

: Weakness Policy can be found on bulky setup Stored Power sets to boost Deoxys-S's power tremendously.

Tera

Due to its unparalleled Speed, great movepool, and abundance of viable setup moves, Deoxys-S is essentially never allowed to use Tera. If one were to make Deoxys-S their Tera Captain, they could use Tera types such as Tera Fighting, Fairy, and Ground to give it needed coverage to effectively counter its typical checks. More defensively-oriented Tera types such as Tera Steel, Water, and Dark could also be useful to facilitate setup sets. Tera Ghost could also be used to deny opposing Rapid Spin on entry hazard lead sets.

Draft Strategy

Deoxys-S is an incredibly versatile Pokemon that has a defined and strong role in almost any given matchup. It can function as an excellent supportive piece to your team with its speed control, entry hazard setting, dual screens setting, and wide utility. If supported well, it can become a centerpiece of your draft with its ability to set up and clean teams.
