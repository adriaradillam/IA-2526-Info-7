Amoonguss has a great defensive profile compared to most other Toxic Spikes absorbers, and this set can help it even further with a slow switch to another teammate once per game.

Pivoting with Leaf Storm

: After using Leaf Storm, Eject Pack activates and Amoonguss is switched out; however, it can also be activated by other stat drops, such as Intimidate and Special Defense drops from Shadow Ball.

Sludge Bomb versus Clear Smog

: Sludge Bomb is preferred for breaking Tera Fairy

Wo-Chien

Chi-Yu

Bold versus Relaxed

: Using Relaxed with a Speed IV of 0 gives Amoonguss the slowest possible pivoting, mostly against mirror matches. However, if both are Terastallized, this means the slower Amoonguss could get hit by Spore, and Amoonguss is already slower than the vast majority of the metagame with Bold.

With good bulk, a solid defensive typing, the ability to absorb Toxic Spikes, and access to Spore and Regenerator, Amoonguss is a fantastic support and disruptor option.

Strategy Comments

Amoonguss mainly works as a bulky support Pokemon. Ideally, it can find value at any point in the game, as healing from Sitrus Berry and Regenerator let it stay healthy.

Amoonguss's presence on a team works to force the opponent to bring Pokemon that cannot be put to sleep. This can be taken advantage of by not bringing Amoonguss game one of a set but bringing it game two where the opponent could respect it less.

Amoonguss's biggest checks are Pokemon that it cannot put to sleep with Spore, whether it's Gholdengo, Grass-types like Rillaboom and opposing Amoonguss, or Safety Goggles Pokemon like Incineroar and Kingambit. This can be played around by pivoting around Spore immunities with Amoonguss to try and find a time to put an important Pokemon to sleep.

Amoonguss serves as the tier's prime disruptor thanks to its various powerful support moves alongside Regenerator, which can also be used by its ally to regain HP upon switching out. With its bulk, it can redirect and absorb various hits by using Rage Powder, as well as threatening to debilitate foes with Spore. Pollen Puff heals Amoonguss's ally even under the effects of Taunt, and Clear Smog negates attempts to set up against Amoonguss. Protect can be used over Clear Smog if Amoonguss's team has ample ways of beating setup Pokemon or does not have enough protection moves to share. Sitrus Berry is best for aiding in Amoonguss's longevity, allowing it to take more hits while redirecting attacks or spreading sleep. The EV spread allows it to survive two uses of Bleakwind Storm from Timid

Tornadus

with Sitrus Berry, and 28 Speed IVs allow it to underspeed base 50 Speed Pokemon with minimum investment such as

Kingambit

and

Iron Hands

. Unfortunately, Amoonguss struggles against opposing Grass-types and

Gholdengo

because of their immunity to Spore, so teammates like

and

Landorus-T

are welcome to prevent Gholdengo from being on the battlefield. Amoonguss also struggles against Pokemon with Safety Goggles, so it appreciates Pokemon like

Incineroar

that can remove said item with Knock Off to help improve Spore's utility. Tera Water allows Amoonguss to resist Fire- and Ice-type attacks from

Chien-Pao

and Incineroar while preserving its resistance against Water-type attacks, while Tera Dark gives it an immunity to Psychic-type attacks from Pokemon like

Hoopa-U

and provides an immunity to Prankster-boosted moves, most importantly Taunt from Tornadus.

Overview

: Amoonguss acts as a status-spreading wall, taking advantage of Regenerator, Spore, and Toxic. Its Poison typing grants it some valuable resistances, the ability to absorb Toxic Spikes, and an advantage against many Spore-immune Pokemon. It can also be rather difficult for many Pokemon to 2HKO thanks to its solid mixed defenses. Unfortunately, its suspect attacking stats and movepool leave it with very little offensive pressure and set diversity. It's for this reason that it becomes incredibly hard to fit onto drafts, as it struggles to keep up with the offensive, fast-paced nature of the metagame.

Common Roles

Wall

: Amoonguss nearly always attempts to wall a number of specific Pokemon on the opponent's draft and spread status as it does so. It can easily remain healthy throughout games thanks to Regenerator, Synthesis, and Giga Drain.

Eject Button Pivot

: Eject Button allows Amoonguss to act as a one-time safe pivot into a teammate. After using its item, it can still function as a regular wall.

Common Items

Eject Button

: Amoonguss can often take one hit from a foe without fear. Eject Button capitalizes on this by bringing in a more threatening teammate. Regenerator keeps Amoonguss healthy after this interaction, so it can remain a roadblock to foes.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Spikes can wear down Amoonguss quickly despite Regenerator unless it has Heavy-Duty Boots.

Black Sludge

: Black Sludge keeps Amoonguss healthy, making it harder to KO. It tends to be interchangeable with Heavy-Duty Boots, but it can be less consistent against hazard-stacking teams.

Rocky Helmet

: Rocky Helmet provides extra damage to chip down opposing physical attackers.

Niche Items

Resistance Berries

: Resistance Berries are used to let Amoonguss lure in wallbreakers that would normally be able to net a KO on it, then put them to sleep with Spore.

Assault Vest

: Amoonguss can more consistently check some special attackers like

Primarina

and

Hydrapple

with the help of an Assault Vest. However, this comes at the cost of not letting it use Spore or other status moves.

Red Card

: Clear Smog and status can stop opposing setup attempts, but some Pokemon are immune. Red Card is a similar option that works on all foes, provided Amoonguss can survive a hit.

Tera

Amoonguss should usually not be considered as a Tera Captain due to its lack of offensive presence. However, Tera types like Tera Water, Fairy, and Steel allow it to wall more foes than it would normally be able to. As such, it can be an acceptable secondary Tera Captain if paired with a threatening attacker that uses Tera most games.
