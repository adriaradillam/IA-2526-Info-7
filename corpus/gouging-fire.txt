Overview

: Gouging Fire uses its great Attack and high natural bulk to be a powerful physical wallbreaker and sweeper; the combination of these traits allows it to match up well against many top-tier Pokemon. With Dragon Dance, Gouging Fire can easily clean games, while Morning Sun gives it longevity against bulkier teams and lets it fill more of a defensive role. It can also use Burning Bulwark to punish revenge killing attempts. However, Gouging Fire's biggest drawback is its at-best decent Speed, leaving it outsped by many Pokemon prior to setting up and many Choice Scarf users at +1.

Common Roles

Setup Sweeper

: Gouging Fire can set up with Dragon Dance to either tear holes in the opposition or clean late-game with its nearly unresisted STAB combination; these sets typically use Booster Energy to help it perform this role better compared to other items.

Choice Band

: In matchups where Gouging Fire has limited setup opportunities, it can instead use a Choice Band to immediately maximize damage dealt. However, its Speed often leaves it outsped by many of the opponent's threats that could OHKO it or deal enough damage to allow another Pokemon to OHKO it.

Physically Defensive

: While more niche, Gouging Fire can use its high HP and Defense alongside useful utility moves like Burning Bulwark, Dragon Tail, and Morning Sun to switch into powerful physical attackers.

: Mental Herb allows Gouging Fire to freely set up against most Pokemon that carry Taunt or Encore.

Resistance Berries

: Gouging Fire can use resistance Berries to survive super effective attacks and either set up on or KO the Pokemon in front of it.

Weakness Policy

: Gouging Fire has the bulk to survive many super effective Dragon-, Ground-, and Rock-type attacks, allowing it to boost its offenses to deadly levels after activating Weakness Policy.

Loaded Dice

: If running Scale Shot, Loaded Dice can be used to make its damage more consistent.

: Lum Berry allows Gouging Fire to absorb a Thunder Wave and ignore confusion from Raging Fury and Outrage.

Tera

Gouging Fire is typically not allowed to Terastallize, but if it can, then it should always be made a Tera Captain. Tera Fairy and Water can be used defensively to get rid of all of its weaknesses and allow it to set up more easily; Tera Fairy gives it Dragon immunity and Tera Water has few weaknesses while still resisting key types. Offensive Tera types can also be used instead; Tera Ground can be used to boost the power of Earthquake, while Tera Fire and Dragon can increase the power of Gouging Fire's STAB moves.
