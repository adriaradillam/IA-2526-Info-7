Set Details

Flutter Mane's role is to capitalize on the Speed boost provided by its sun setter and to offer speed control while pressuring opposing Pokemon to Terastallize with its coverage. In sun, it outspeeds major offensive threats like

Miraidon

,

Koraidon

Calyrex-S

Shadow Ball is useful for threatening

Taunt is a useful tool for shutting down opposing Trick Room setters like Lunala and

Calyrex-I

Amoonguss

and

Terapagos

Becuase it usually has Koraidon to set the sun for its Protosynthesis Speed boost, this Flutter Mane set can run full offensive investment and a Focus Sash instead of Booster Energy. Focus Sash is valuable for this more offensive Flutter Mane build due to its fragility, especially on the physical side.

Tera Normal is mainly used as a countermeasure for Calyrex-S to gain an immunity to its Astral Barrage, though it can also be useful to gain an immunity to Lunala's Moongeist Beam. Tera Fairy is used to boost Moonblast's power, which lets it OHKO non-Assault Vest Miraidon outright as well as Tera Dragon

Zamazenta-C

and Brute Bonnet most of the time. Tera Stellar carries a similar benefit to Tera Fairy in terms of Moonblast OHKOs, though only on its first use, and it can also enable Shadow Ball to score surprise OHKOs on Pokemon such as Shadow Shield Lunala when

Chi-Yu

is on the field.

Teambuilding

Bulky Restricted Pokemon like Zamazenta-C and

Ho-Oh

check this offensive Flutter Mane set, as they can comfortably take its hits and strike back with moves like Behemoth Bash or Heavy Slam and Sacred Fire, respectively.

Priority move users such as

Flutter Mane pairs well with Koraidon because it sets up the sun, triggering Flutter Mane's Protosynthesis Speed boost. In turn, Flutter Mane can provide speed control with Icy Wind, so Koraidon is able to outspeed Pokemon like Calyrex-S and threaten OHKOs with Close Combat or Flare Blitz.

Flutter Mane is commonly paired with Choice Scarf Chi-Yu to round out a sun core because both Flutter Mane and Chi-Yu exert fast, immediate offensive pressure, with Chi-Yu's ability helping bolster Flutter Mane's damage output.

Flutter Mane is an effective fast attacker. A Ghost typing and good Speed let it effectively use Normalize + Entrainment as a disruption tool, while more offensive sets can benefit from Moongeist Beam for high damage against Ice Scales users like

Ho-Oh

Flutter Mane's weak Defense also means it can't afford to stay in on stray hits like Flip Turn much, either.

Thanks to a combination of its great stat distribution and typing, this set turns Flutter Mane into an exceptional utility Pokemon capable of exerting long-term pressure. Magic Guard mitigates Life Orb recoil, poison damage, and entry hazard damage. Alternatively, Pixilate provides Flutter Mane with an extremely spammable STAB Boomburst, which, even without a boosting item, is capable of putting pressure to force special walls in, while Teravolt allows Flutter Mane to bypass Ice Scales and Magic Bounce. Utility Flutter Mane works well on balanced structures, though it can also perform on more offensive teams. Almost all offensive Pokemon appreciate Flutter Mane's support with Spikes. Taunt shuts off annoyances like Glare and Parting Shot, and it additionally pairs well with all last-move options; Nasty Plot + Taunt makes it a solid sweeper with the rare ability to beat Topsy-Turvy users, Spikes is especially useful due to Entrainment beating all forms of entry hazard removal and forcing switches, and Thunder Cage + Taunt allows it to trap and remove targets that lack ways to damage it through Normalize. Flutter Mane's best fit is on balance teams that can, depending on its set, either take advantage of its Spikes-setting ability or help pressure special walls that can beat Normalize.

Other Options

Flutter Mane's immunities to Dragon and Fighting mean it can use defensive abilities like Ice Scales and immunity abilities like Well-Baked Body and Levitate; however, this eats into damage output, making Magic Bounce users much more problematic. Flutter Mane can potentially use a Choice Specs set with either Beads of Ruin or Pixilate, but its damage falls short of competitors like

Mega Alakazam
