Overview

: Ogerpon-W functions as a strong wallbreaker and sweeper, especially as a Tera Captain. It has a good STAB combination and solid Attack stat, which can be further boosted by Swords Dance and Terastallization. It also has a good Speed tier that can be amplified with Trailblaze. It also has solid support options, such as Encore, Knock Off, Spikes and U-turn, which can make it useful in matchups where it has a hard time wallbreaking. Despite its immense power, Ogerpon-W is significantly less flexible than other high-priced Tera Captains, being unable to change its item or Tera type. In addition, bulky Grass- and Dragon-types, as well as some opposing Tera users, can be difficult to muscle past.

Common Roles

Setup Wallbreaker

: Ogerpon-W excels at setting up Swords Dance and putting immediate pressure on the opposing team with its extremely powerful STAB moves. It has a good variety of coverage options like Knock Off, Play Rough, and Superpower to break past its checks. In addition, the Special Defense boost from Embody Aspect can come in handy to survive a special hit from a faster Pokemon that would ordinarily revenge kill Ogerpon-W. Depending on the matchup, Ogerpon-W can make use of utility moves such as Synthesis and Encore to give it more opportunities to setup and wallbreak.

Trailblaze Cleaner

: In certain matchups where Ogerpon-W is not expected to sweep, Ogerpon-W can function as a potential late-game cleaner after using Trailblaze and using Tera-boosted Ivy Cudgel to blow past the rest of the opposing team once it is sufficiently weakened.

Entry Hazard Utility

: In matchups where Ogerpon-W would find it difficult to wallbreak or sweep, it can fulfill a more supporting role of setting up Spikes to pressure the opposing team and assist team members. In conjunction with Leech Seed, Horn Leech, and Synthesis for recovery; Knock Off and Encore for disruption; and U-turn for pivoting, Ogerpon-W can always accomplish something meaningful in a given matchup.
