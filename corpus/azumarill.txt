Overview

: Azumarill fits onto teams as a powerful bulky wallbreaker with excellent offensive and defensive typing, able to absorb attacks and fire back with force due to Huge Power, strong STAB options, and priority in Aqua Jet. Although it requires a very calculated game state, Azumarill is the premier Belly Drum sweeper in the meta and forces preparation accordingly. Despite these strengths, Azumarill is not a perfect fit on every roster, as its lack of longevity, low Speed for an offensive Pokemon, and general difficulty to position make it a flawed Pokemon. This combination of weaknesses and the prevalence of other desirable Water- or Fairy-type Pokemon cause Azumarill to be undrafted regularly or taken in the later rounds if it fits a particular roster.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Azumarill can use a Choice Band or damage-boosting item to output large amounts of damage and break down opposing walls. These sets can also handle faster or weakened foes with a powerful priority Aqua Jet to become a late-game cleaning threat.

Belly Drum Sweeper

: Azumarill can maximize its damage output and become a potentially game-ending sweeper by using Belly Drum. These sets rely on Sitrus Berry to get a Belly Drum off while retaining HP and can sweep once Aqua Jet cannot be checked by foes or the opposing teams lacks priority options to revenge kill Azumarill. Trailblaze can also be used to outspeed slightly faster Pokemon that could normally check and revenge kill it.

Sap Sipper Wall

: Azumarill can use Sap Sipper to become a more defensively-oriented team member capable of beating Grass-type foes. These sets are anti-meta, as they check many top threats and can often surprise a foe, trap them with Whirlpool, and beat them one-on-one through a variety of methods such as Perish Song.

: Endure can be used to ensure Azumarill survives an attack and activates Custap Berry to dish out an additional powerful attack.

Common Items

Choice Band

: Choice Band is a premier item for Azumarill to let it wallbreak with potency and weaken nearly any foe tremendously. It also makes Azumarill's Aqua Jet into a great late-game cleaning option and win condition.

Damage-boosting Items

: Similarly to Choice Band, items such as Mystic Water and Expert Belt allow Azumarill to hit higher damage thresholds to tear down opposing walls. These items are used when Azumarill wishes to switch attacks, which can be important given its ability to hit a foe into KO range and then clean up with a priority Aqua Jet.

Sitrus Berry

: With even HP, a Sitrus Berry, and a dream, Azumarill can use Belly Drum in a favorable field position and then regain HP to facilitate a potential sweep. This item can also have more general applications to help Azumarill avoid being KOed in certain situations and provide it extra longevity.

Assault Vest

: Azumarill can take advantage of its combination of strong STAB attacks, priority Aqua Jet, utility attacks like Knock Off, and coverage to effectively use an Assault Vest and gain the bulk to sponge hits from and check some special threats.

Niche Items

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Heavy-Duty Boots provides Azumarill safety from getting chipped down by entry hazards. On average, Heavy-Duty Boots will be more consistent than healing items for promoting its longevity, due to the prevalence of hazard-stacking teams.

Leftovers

: Leftovers provide extra longevity to Azumarill which greatly appreciates passive healing if it is not too threatened by hazards in a given matchup.

Resistance Berries

: Resistance Berries such as Wacan and Rindo Berry will let Azumarill unexpectedly absorb one super effective attack to trade damage with a foe or gain extra security.

Lum Berry

: Lum Berry can be effective in preventing a burn that would greatly diminish Azumarill's damage output.

Rocky Helmet

: Rocky Helmet can be found on bulkier sets to punish contact moves, as Azumarill can capably absorb common contact attacks such as Knock Off and U-turn due to its Fairy typing.

Custap Berry

: Custap Berry is used on sets in conjunction with Endure to allow Azumarill to ignore its poor Speed and gain an extra chance to attack.

Tera

Azumarill is not an ideal primary Tera Captain, as it already has excellent typing, can only set up in very specific scenarios due to its low Speed, and generally is outclassed by other potential Tera Captains. If you make Azumarill a Tera Captain, it often uses STAB Tera types to further enhance its wallbreaking capabilities and the power of its strong Aqua Jet. It can also use defensive Tera types such as Tera Grass, Electric, and Poison to flip type matchups in its favor and ease its ability to use Belly Drum safely.
