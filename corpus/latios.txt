Overview

: With a great Special Attack stat, powerful STAB moves and coverage, and a good Speed stat, Latios can be a solid wallbreaker and cleaner. It also has access to utility options like Trick and Thunder Wave, which it can use to catch the opponent off guard and potentially hinder a foe. However, Latios gets threatened by the many faster foes that can easily deal lots of damage to it with moves such as Knock Off and U-turn. Additionally, it is often reliant on Choice items for additional damage or Speed, making it easy to take advantage of.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: With Choice Specs and powerful STAB moves in Draco Meteor, Luster Purge, and Psyshock, Latios can rip holes in teams. Aura Sphere allows it to break through most Steel-types that would otherwise resist its STAB combination.

Speed Control

: Latios's good Speed stat lets it use a Choice Scarf to outspeed and threaten a plethora of offensive threats, making it a good revenge killer and late-game cleaner. It can also get around its checks by pivoting with Flip Turn or crippling them with Trick.

Setup Sweeper

: Latios can use a setup set with Agility to outspeed the opponent's offensive threats. Calm Mind can also be useful against bulkier teams, with the Special Defense boost helping it survive attacks better. While unreliable, Weakness Policy can let Latios boost to deadly levels off of a single attack.

Common Items

Choice Specs

: Choice Specs is Latios's best item if it is acting as a wallbreaker, letting it do massive damage without the need to set up.

Choice Scarf

: With Choice Scarf, Latios can outspeed and threaten many offensive Pokemon, including many setup sweepers at +1, making it a great revenge killer.

Life Orb

: While not quite as strong as Choice Specs, Life Orb allows Latios to switch between its moves at the cost of recoil damage.

Resistance Berries

: Calm Mind sets can use resistance Berries to grant Latios more setup opportunities; Colbur Berry is a great option for decreasing Knock Off's power.

Niche Items

Soul Dew

: Soul Dew can be used to increase the power of Latios's STAB moves at the cost of the additional damage Choice Specs and Life Orb provides.

Weakness Policy

: Weakness Policy can be used on setup sets alongside Stored Power and Agility to boost Latios to deadly levels in a single turn.

Eject Pack

: Latios can use an Eject Pack with Draco Meteor to pivot out while dealing significant damage to an incoming wall.

Mental Herb

: Mental Herb allows Latios to freely set up against most Taunt and Encore users.

Lum Berry

: Lum Berry can be used on Calm Mind sets as a one-time means to absorb status moves like Thunder Wave and Toxic.

Light Clay

: If Latios is running dual screens, Light Clay can be used to further support its teammates.

Tera

Latios is usually banned from Terastallizing due to its versatile setup options, great defensive ability, and great offensive coverage. In instances where Latios is allowed to Terastallize, Tera Dragon and Psychic can increase the power of its STAB moves, but other Tera types can be used for necessary coverage moves. Defensively, Tera Fairy removes many of Latios's weaknesses, and Tera Steel, Electric, and Poison synergize well defensively with Levitate.
