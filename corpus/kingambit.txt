Kingambit shines as one of the tier's most dangerous sweepers thanks to its solid STAB combination, superb defensive typing, high Attack stat, access to Swords Dance, and incredible ability in Supreme Overlord. Its access to Sucker Punch allows it to compensate for its low Speed and serve as a potent revenge killer, picking off a wide range of faster targets such as

Dragapult

,

Deoxys-S

, and

Iron Moth

. It can also customize its EV spread, item choice, and Tera type selection to tailor itself to what its team desires and overcome many would-be answers. For example, Tera Flying and Air Balloon improve Kingambit's matchup against Ground-types, Tera Fire and Lum Berry let it foil attempted burns from Will-O-Wisp, and Tera Ghost flips its Fighting weakness and turns the tables on Body Press

Corviknight

and Fighting-types like

Zamazenta

and

Great Tusk

. Thanks to these traits and flexibility, Kingambit is welcomed by nearly every offensive archetype in the tier. However, it is heavily burdened by a crippling weakness to Fighting, as well as common attacking types like Fire and Ground. This leaves it struggling against common threats like Zamazenta and Great Tusk without Terastallizing, and plenty of Pokemon like

Gholdengo

, Deoxys-S, and

Iron Crown

often slap on Fighting-type coverage to deal with it. Additionally, these Pokemon, as well as Corviknight and

Dondozo

, often utilize Tera Fighting specifically to turn the tables on Kingambit. Tera Fairy is also commonplace in the tier, as Pokemon like Gholdengo,

Raging Bolt

, and Iron Moth sometimes utilize it in part to avoid being revenge killed by Kingambit's Dark-type attacks. Additionally, Kingambit's low Speed forces it to rely on Sucker Punch to deal with faster teams and play carefully around Encore from foes such as

Ogerpon-W

,

Dragonite

, and

Iron Valiant

. Relying on Sucker Punch also makes Kingambit vulnerable to well-timed status moves such as Will-O-Wisp, Substitute, and recovery moves from Pokemon like

Darkrai

, Dragapult,

Kyurem

, and Iron Moth. The ubiquity of priority moves, such as Raging Bolt's Thunderclap and Dragonite's Extreme Speed, is also an issue for Kingambit's Sucker Punch. On top of this, Kingambit lacks reliable recovery, and it struggles to fit all the moves it wants in one set to threaten targets like Great Tusk, opposing Kingambit, and

Hisuian Samurott

.

Kingambit is a potent late-game cleaner with Swords Dance, Sucker Punch, and Supreme Overlord. Kingambit can drop one of its STAB moves for Low Kick, which hits

Hisuian Samurott

and opposing Kingambit super effectively while netting crucial benchmarks such as potentially OHKOing

Ting-Lu

at +2, especially because common Fighting-resistant Pokemon like

Galarian Slowking

and

Pecharunt

want nothing to do with Kingambit; Low Kick should only be used on maximum Speed variants to try and hit opposing Kingambit first. However, dropping Iron Head means it is walled by Fairy-types and Tera Fairy users such as

Clefable

,

Galarian Weezing

,

Gholdengo

, and

Moltres

, while dropping Kowtow Cleave makes Kingambit struggle to beat neutral walls like

Corviknight

,

Alomomola

, and

Zapdos

. Leftovers takes further advantage of its defensive profile and works well with Sucker Punch, as foes attempting to play around it will just grant Kingambit more health. Leftovers is especially more effective on variants running the bulky EV spread. Lum Berry mainly prevents Kingambit from being shut down by a burn from the likes of

Dragapult

,

Darkrai

, Alomomola, and Moltres. Black Glasses + Tera Dark should exclusively be used together with Kowtow Cleave, as these propel Kingambit's offensive pressure further; with five Supreme Overlord boosts under its belt, Kingambit can reach feats such as 2HKOing even

Dondozo

with Kowtow Cleave and OHKOing

Dragonite

through Multiscale with Sucker Punch at +2. Air Balloon allows Kingambit to set up on would-be checks in

Landorus-T

and most

Great Tusk

; become a sturdy switch-in to offensive threats like

Kyurem

and Dragonite that mostly rely on their Ground-type coverage to hit it; and gain an immunity to Spikes, which is one of the ways to limit Kingambit's health before it gets going. The latter reasoning thus makes Heavy-Duty Boots an appealing alternative option to combat entry hazards in general, especially on hazard-stacking teams that lack reliable hazard removers while aiming to play a longer game. Similar to Air Balloon, Shuca Berry can be used instead for its matchup against the aforementioned Ground-types and Ground coverage moves; it gives Kingambit more freedom to take on other attacks before if necessary and it hides Kingambit's item from the opponent at the cost of a complete immunity to Ground, even against Spikes. Tera Ghost flips its Fighting weakness, letting it go toe-to-toe against otherwise solid checks in IronPress users like Corviknight,

Skarmory

, and

Zamazenta

more reliably, especially because it naturally puts fear in the likes of Dragapult and Gholdengo from trying to exploit its newfound Ghost weakness. Tera Fighting can be used on Low Kick variants, boosting the move to pressure Ting-Lu, Dondozo, and Great Tusk hard. Tera Flying is an option for its complete immunity to Ground moves from checks like Great Tusk and Landorus-T, especially on sets without Air Balloon. Tera Fire provides a burn immunity from foes like Dragapult, Darkrai, and Moltres, even flipping its Fire weakness against the latter and other threats like

Cinderace

and

Iron Moth

. An Adamant nature is preferred, with maximum Speed investment to Speed tie opposing Kingambit; going even further with a Jolly nature gives better odds in winning this interaction, which also helps in outspeeding most variants of Skarmory,

Primarina

, and

Sinistcha

. A bulkier EV spread further takes advantage of Kingambit's hit-taking abilities, remarkably reaching feats such as surviving +2

Ogerpon-W

's Ivy Cudgel and two

Iron Valiant

Moonblasts while running just enough Speed for

Blissey

.

Kingambit's late-game potential and defensive utility make it a near staple on any offensive archetype, ranging from bulky to hyper offense, while still being capable of fitting on balance teams. Physically offensive teammates like Ogerpon-W, Zamazenta, Dragonite, and Swords Dance Iron Valiant are helpful to overwhelm shared checks like Zamazenta, Landorus-T, Moltres, and Dondozo. Simultaneously, special attackers like Calm Mind Iron Valiant, Gholdengo, Dragapult, Iron Moth, Kyurem, and

Raging Bolt

are similarly helpful to handle and pressure these checks, preventing them from getting free turns to set up or recover health. Additionally, their synergy doesn't stop there, as Zamazenta and some Iron Valiant variants appreciate Kingambit's ability to switch into and threaten Gholdengo and Galarian Slowking; Dragonite can take on Iron Moth and provide a Ground immunity against Landorus-T; Gholdengo can switch into and check Zamazenta for Kingambit; Dragapult can check Zamazenta and bring Kingambit safely via U-turn; and Kyurem and Raging Bolt can threaten and force damage, respectively, onto Kingambit's Ground-type checks in Landorus-T, Great Tusk, and Ting-Lu. Dark-type offensive threats in Darkrai and Hisuian Samurott, in particular, are notable in forming a Dark-type spam core together with Kingambit, which is capable of overwhelming Dark-resistant Pokemon like Zamazenta and Tera Fairy users like Gholdengo,

Garganacl

, and Moltres together. Moreover, the aforementioned Hisuian Samurott helps chip foes into Kingambit's KO range with its entry hazards, whether it's softening walls like Ting-Lu or potential revenge killers like Ogerpon-W and Iron Moth into Sucker Punch range; thus, other hazard setters like Landorus-T,

Glimmora

,

Deoxys-S

, and Ting-Lu are similarly valuable. Defensive pivots like Landorus-T, Zapdos, Pecharunt, and Galarian Slowking are common faces on bulky offense teams that help switch into threats like Cinderace, Landorus-T, Great Tusk, and Zamazenta for Kingambit and are great at bringing it in safely onto the field, especially with Intimidate and Parting Shot to ease its setup; in return, Kingambit helps check Darkrai, Gholdengo, and Dragapult for Galarian Slowking and Pecharunt. Other and more specific offensive playstyles like Sticky Web, dual screens, and the fringe Trick Room can also mesh well with Kingambit. Sticky Web from

Ribombee

and

Araquanid

compensates for Kingambit's low Speed against faster threats like Raging Bolt, Hisuian Samurott, and Great Tusk if Kingambit runs a Jolly nature, even making it less reliant on Sucker Punch mind games. Dual screens from

Alolan Ninetales

give Kingambit an easier time setting up and let it stomach even super effective hits from Iron Moth and Darkrai. Similar to Sticky Web, Trick Room set by teammates like

Hatterene

can compensate for Kingambit's poor Speed tier, especially against fast foes like Iron Valiant and Encore Ogerpon-W. On balance teams, a sturdier defensive backbone with the likes of Skarmory, Corviknight, Clefable,

Gliscor

, and Alomomola is similarly appreciated, as on top of switching into Kingambit's aforementioned checks and revenge killers, they can also set entry hazards and bring it in safely via slow pivots; Alomomola can also provide Kingambit with Wish support, while Gliscor and Clefable can further weaken walls with Knock Off. Outside of its utility set, Swords Dance Gliscor, in particular, is also a strong setup sweeper that complements Kingambit well.

Other Options

Kingambit has a few other Tera types that it can make use of. Tera Grass flips the matchup against

Ogerpon-W

and lets Kingambit set up on Ground-types like

Great Tusk

,

Ting-Lu

, and

Iron Treads

. Tera Poison grants Kingambit a Fighting resistance while keeping its immunity to Toxic and has the benefit of absorbing Toxic Spikes for teams that are otherwise at a disadvantage against them. Black Belt can be used on Tera Fighting + Low Kick sets, with the boost in particular letting it 2HKO

Dondozo

with five Supreme Overlord boosts. Clear Amulet is another item option to ignore

Landorus-T

's Intimidate and disrupt Parting Shot from

Pecharunt

to stop it from switching out. Thunder Wave can be used to cripple incoming switch-ins, such as

Zamazenta

and

Moltres

, and it synergizes well with Iron Head's chance to flinch.

With the help of Supreme Overlord and strong priority in Sucker Punch, Kingambit serves as an excellent late-game sweeper. Its typing also grants it the ability to soft check

Calyrex-S

and Extreme Killer

Arceus

, two of the most prominent Pokemon in AG. Kowtow Cleave gives Kingambit a reliable Dark-type STAB move and lets it hit

Ho-Oh

,

Miraidon

, and

Kyogre

harder on the switch. Low Kick can be used instead to better check Arceus and hit

Ting-Lu

harder, OHKOing Arceus after a Swords Dance and Ting-Lu with one Supreme Overlord boost, while also allowing Kingambit to check opposing Kingambit. Leftovers improves Kingambit's longevity and improves its ability to check Calyrex-S and Extreme Killer Arceus, (AC) while Black Glasses increases Kingambit's damage even further, notably giving Sucker Punch a very good chance to OHKO 120 HP Miraidon with two Supreme Overlord boosts and a Swords Dance. Air Balloon can be used instead to give Kingambit an easier time versus Ground-type Pokemon such as

Arceus-Ground

, Ting-Lu, and

Landorus-T

by giving it a temporary immunity to their super effective Ground-type attacks. Kingambit also has great flexibility with its Tera type. Tera Fire gives Kingambit an immunity to burn and a resistance to Fire-type attacks from

Koraidon

and Ho-Oh; it also lets Kingambit avoid an OHKO from Koraidon's Low Kick from full HP. On the other hand, Tera Dark boosts Kingambit's Dark-type attacks to ridiculous levels and can be used in conjunction with Black Glasses to give Kingambit's +2 Sucker Punch a 50% chance to OHKO Koraidon after one layer of Spikes and five boosts of Supreme Overlord. Tera Flying is yet another option that gives Kingambit an immunity to Ground-type attacks from Arceus-Ground and Ting-Lu and a resistance to Fighting-type attacks from Koraidon and

Zacian-C

. Kingambit must be wary of Terastallizing, however, as it removes its resistance to Arceus's Extreme Speed, making it vulnerable to being revenge killed. The given EV spread allows Kingambit to outspeed uninvested

Skeledirge

.

Due to its lack of recovery, Kingambit fits best on offensive teams that appreciate its ability to check Calyrex-S and Extreme Killer Arceus short-term, as well as its efficacy into Sticky Web teams due to its strong priority. Funnily enough, Calyrex-S and Extreme Killer Arceus also make some of Kingambit's best partners. Calyrex-S appreciates Kingambit's ability to check opposing Arceus and, in return, helps break through Ting-Lu and Ho-Oh. Meanwhile, Arceus creates a strong priority spam core with Kingambit that overwhelms their shared checks such as Zacian-C, Ho-Oh, and defensive Arceus formes. Arceus and Kingambit also both check Calyrex-S, which is helpful because it helps avoid bad situations created by Encore. Kingambit can also benefit from the fact that Arceus often forces the opponent to Terastallize their Koraidon into the Ghost-type, which makes it easier for Kingambit to sweep late-game. Other physical wallbreakers such as Koraidon and Zacian-C also have excellent synergy with Kingambit, as they can help wear down opposing physical walls such as the aforementioned Ho-Oh and defensive Arceus formes as well as Landorus-T. Swords Dance Koraidon in particular is a great partner, since it often forces Ho-Oh to Terastallize into the Fairy-type, which makes Ho-Oh vulnerable to Iron Head. In return, Kingambit can switch into Calyrex-S and Arceus trying to revenge kill Koraidon. As Kingambit is often forced out by Fire- and Fighting- type attacks from wallbreakers such as Koraidon and Zacian-C, a strong defensive core is necessary. Landorus-T can switch into both Zacian-C and Koraidon and rack up chip damage on them with Rocky Helmet and Stealth Rock. Stealth Rock also helps chip down walls like support Arceus formes and opposing Landorus-T. Defensive Arceus formes like

Arceus-Fairy

and

Arceus-Water

can also switch into these threats and spread burn, which can help rack up chip damage and make it easier for Kingambit to set up. Ting-Lu switches into Miraidon and Arceus-Ground, acts as another check to Calyrex-S, and can set up Spikes that helps break down defensive Arceus formes and Koraidon over the course the game.

Other Options

Mental Herb can be used to allow Kingambit to set up in front of Pokemon running Taunt or to avoid getting locked into an unfavorable move by Encore

Calyrex-S

. Lum Berry is also another option that allows Kingambit to avoid getting burned by defensive

Arceus

formes and

Skeledirge

without having to Terastallize. Defiant can also be used to help against Sticky Web teams and

Landorus-T

Checks and Counters

Dark-resistant Pokemon

:

Koraidon

and

Zacian

can take Kingambit's Dark-type attacks fairly well and threaten an OHKO with their strong Fighting- and Fire-type attacks, usually forcing Kingambit to switch out.

Bulky Ground-types

:

Ting-Lu

,

Arceus-Ground

, and Landorus-T can all take an attack from Kingambit and threaten it back with their super effective STAB moves. However, they must be wary of Tera Flying.

Physical Walls

: Physical walls like defensive Arceus formes and

Ho-Oh

can switch into Kingambit's attacks and cripple it with burn, though they must be wary of Tera Fire.

Encore Calyrex-S

: If Kingambit isn't running Mental Herb, then Calyrex-S can use Encore to lock it into Swords Dance or Sucker Punch and set up Nasty Plot for free.

Supreme Overlord, a high Attack stat, access to Swords Dance and Sucker Punch, and a wide variety of items and Tera types at its disposal make Kingambit one of the most dangerous late-game sweepers in the tier, being able to use Terastallization to lure in and beat Pokemon that would normally check it and completely take games at the drop of a hat. It also has an amazing defensive typing that lets it easily set up on metagame staples like

Lunala

and

Necrozma-DM

to start its sweep. However, the abundance of common Dark-resistant Pokemon in the tier makes making complete comebacks harder, as Pokemon like

Koraidon

,

Zacian-C

, and

Arceus-Fairy

, among others, can take a Sucker Punch and end its attempt at a sweep either by OHKOing it or burning it, though this can be circumvented with Terastallization. Kingambit is also forced into not coming in for the majority of the game, as it's heavily reliant on using up its HP or Terastallization to sweep, and when no teammates have fainted, it is weak compared to the rest of the metagame.

Every part of this set aims to make sure Kingambit can finish off the opposing team with Sucker Punch, as due to the massive damage output of Ubers, Kingambit only gets one chance to sweep. Black Glasses makes Kingambit's Dark-type moves hit astronomically hard, especially with Tera Dark, being able to OHKO Tera Fire

Calyrex-I

and

Zacian-C

as well as

Koraidon

with minimal chip damage with Sucker Punch after a Swords Dance boost and 5 Supreme Overlord boosts. Air Balloon allows Kingambit to come in on the multitude of Ground-type attacks from

Arceus-Ground

,

Landorus-T

, and

Necrozma-DM

, allowing it an opportunity to set up. Lum Berry allows it to absorb burns from

Ho-Oh

and defensive

Arceus

formes, allowing Kingambit to forgo Tera Fire for another Tera type. Tera Dark powers up Kingambit's Dark- type moves even further, allowing Kingambit to, crucially, OHKO Ho-Oh and Arceus-Ground with a Swords Dance boosted Sucker Punch with 5 Supreme Overlord boosts. Tera Fire grants Kingambit a burn immunity and a Fire resistance while removing its Fighting weakness, which allow it to set up on Pokemon like Ho-Oh, Koraidon, and Zacian-C.

Kingambit fits best on hyper offensive teams that need a late-game cleaner and a revenge killer for Pokemon like Calyrex-I, Necrozma-DM, and

Eternatus

. As it's fairly weak without a significant amount of teammates fainted, entry hazard setters like

and

In terms of offensive teammates, Koraidon and Zacian-C are amazing teammates, as they can wear down or force common checks such as Ho-Oh, Landorus-T, and defensive Arceus forms to Terastallize, allowing Kingambit to sweep later. Extreme Killer Arceus can force Koraidon and

Kyogre

to Terastallize into a Ghost-type, allowing Kingambit to beat them with Sucker Punch, and one can wear down checks like Kyogre and defensive Arceus formes for the other to break through. Necrozma-DM can lure in Ho-Oh and cripple it with either Knock Off or Stone Edge, allowing Kingambit to muscle through. Meteor Beam Eternatus can also force in and damage Ho-Oh while also threatening

Arceus-Fairy

and

Arceus-Water

Other Options

Tera Flying can be used to become immune to Ground, allowing it to constantly come in on Ground attacks from

Arceus-Ground

and

Landorus-T

without sacrificing the item slot for Air Balloon. Tera Ghost can also be used for an immunity to Fighting-type attacks and Extreme Speed, but Kingambit is resistant to Extreme Speed anyway and the Fighting immunity isn't that worth it over other Tera types like Fire. Heavy-Duty Boots can be used to prevent Kingambit from being chipped down by entry hazards over the course of a game on a bulky offense team.

Checks and Counters

Dark-resistant Pokemon

: Pokemon that resist Kingambit's Sucker Punch, like

Koraidon

,

Zacian-C

, and

Arceus-Fairy

, are very common, so Kingambit can have a hard time breaking through these Pokémon. There are also a lot of Pokémon that can Terastallize into a Dark-resistant type, like

Lunala

and

Ho-Oh

, among others.

Ho-Oh

: Ho-Oh can easily shrug off Kingambit's Sucker Punches, outspeed it, and retaliate with Sacred Fire. Kingambit can circumvent this with Lum Berry or Tera Fire, but it still needs to be wary of Whirlwind.

Defensive

Arceus

Formes

: Arceus-Fairy, Arceus-Ground, and

Arceus-Water

can all easily take Kingambit's attacks and threaten Kingambit with either burn from Will-O-Wisp or damage with Judgement, especially if Kingambit has already Terastallized into a Dark- or Fire-type. Kingambit has ways of playing around these, though, like Lum Berry, Air Balloon, or just pressuring them with entry hazard chip damage.

Landorus-T

: Landorus-T’s Intimidate can make Kingambit thud in the face of it and can use these free turns to set up Stealth Rock, stop it from setting up with Taunt, or just threaten an Earthquake, all while racking up chip damage with Rocky Helmet. However, Landorus-T lacks reliable recovery, so it can get chipped down over the course of the game to the point where Kingambit is able to break through.

Swords Dance, mixed with Kingambit's naturally high Attack as well as access to Defiant to scare off Intimidate users and priority in Sucker Punch, makes it one of the most effective setup sweepers in the metagame. Its respectable bulk, especially its base 120 Defense, often allows it to set up multiple Swords Dances in one match.

Kowtow Cleave offers strong and consistent damage. It OHKOes

Gholdengo

and

Indeedee-F

without a boost and decimates bulky Pokemon like

Amoonguss

and

Entei

at +2, netting guaranteed KOs after minimal chip damage.

Sucker Punch compensates for Kingambit's low Speed, OHKOing faster attackers such as

Landorus

and

Ogerpon-W

after a boost. It also allows Kingambit to revenge kill weakened foes when entering the field.

Dread Plate (or Black Glasses) is vital for Kingambit to secure numerous benchmarks for OHKOs or 2HKOs on Pokemon such as Landorus with Sucker Punch and Ogerpon-W with Kowtow Cleave. Other items can be used in niche scenarios, such as Leftovers for gradual health recovery and Grassy Seed on teams with

Rillaboom

to become resilient to most physical attacks.

Kingambit has a multitude of options for its Tera type. Tera Dark can be used to strengthen its attacks and reduce its weakness to

Iron Hands

's Fighting-type attacks to only 2x while removing its weaknesses to

Incineroar

's Flare Blitz,

Ogerpon-H

's Ivy Cudgel, and Landorus's Earth Power. Tera Dragon allows Kingambit to resist attacks from Ogerpon-H, Ogerpon-W, and

Chi-Yu

. Tera Flying grants an immunity to Landorus's Ground-type moves as well as another way to resist Iron Hands's attacks, but it gives Kingambit a much worse matchup against the faster

Raging Bolt

and

Chien-Pao

.

Kingambit appreciates partners that can create space for it to set up a Swords Dance; this includes Fake Out users, such as Incineroar, Rillaboom, and Iron Hands, and redirection support from the likes of

Sinistcha

and Ogerpon-W. One bonus of running Incineroar is that it actually improves Kingambit's matchup against opposing Incineroar, as it can easily switch into Flare Blitz if necessary or at least reduce the damage Kingambit takes from an opposing Incineroar's Flare Blitz. Landorus fends off some of Kingambit's biggest threats like Iron Hands and Entei, while it appreciates Kingambit's ability to remove some of its threats like Ogerpon-W with a boosted Sucker Punch. Landorus is also one of Kingambit's best offensive checks due to its strong Earth Power, so Kingambit appreciates the support of Jolly Ogerpon-W and Chien-Pao to threaten it offensively.

Kingambit's Defiant supports strong physical attackers such as Rillaboom, Ogerpon-W, and Chien-Pao by deterring opposing Intimidate users. Its Steel typing and opportunity to get a Defiant boost from Bleakwind Storm also make it one of the strongest

Tornadus

checks in the tier, which is appreciated by the aforementioned Grass-types.

Gouging Fire

can further boost Kingambit's Attack with Howl, while Kingambit deters Intimidate users, creating room for multiple turns of boosting.

Strategy Comments

Kingambit's main role is either a setup sweeper with Swords Dance or a defensive pivot piece with Assault Vest. Swords Dance Kingambit's main goal is to set up and stay healthy; redirection and Fake Out support are usually necessary in achieving this. With Tera Dark and at +2, it's very easy for Kingambit to pick up momentum quickly and blitz through careless players. Don't be greedy with using Swords Dances, as it's easy for Kingambit to be OHKOed or brought to low health with careful positioning around Sucker Punch, especially in a format with Fighting-type coverage everywhere.

Kingambit succeeds as a setup sweeper thanks to its typing, bulk, and on Assault Vest sets, coverage. Its typing has many resistances to top types like Dragon, making Kingambit an amazing counter to

Dragapult

and

Hydreigon

. Kingambit's bulk, especially when it's paired with Assault Vest, is really good, allowing Kingambit to withstand hits and potentially turn the tables in a losing late-game position.

Dark-resistant Pokemon are a huge issue for Kingambit, easily taking Kowtow Cleave and Sucker Punch even if they're boosted. These Pokemon can be taken care of by partners such as Primarina and Sneasler. Fighting-types, including the likes of Annihilape, can be a massive issue, easily using Kingambit to set up. Sneasler in particular can be a problem, only taking notable damage from Iron Head. Fighting-type coverage moves are also rather prevalent in the metagame, with Pokemon like Low Kick

Tyranitar

and Body Press

Archaludon

Offensive Fairy-types such as Primarina can scare Kingambit away from Terastallizing into a Dark-type, as Moonblast would otherwise easily chunk Kingambit. Sneasler can easily deal with Fairy-types by hitting them hard with Dire Claw.

Kingambit acts as a valuable sweeper and cleaner on offensive teams, providing a refreshing blend of offensive and defensive utility in the form of strong priority in Sucker Punch and the option of having a valuable type immunity. Swords Dance boosts Kingambit's Attack to nuclear levels, OHKOing physically defensive

Overview

: Kingambit boasts great Attack and two great abilities that make it a terrifying presence when it comes onto the field. Although Supreme Overlord is used more often, Defiant is great to stop Pokemon with Intimidate from checking it reliably. Despite its low Speed, Kingambit has the bulk to take on strong neutral hits and hit back with its good STAB combination and priority, making it tough to remove, especially as a Tera Captain. Furthermore, it has a good number of utility options to support its team if needed. However, like a king on a chessboard, Kingambit requires a lot of support from its teammates to function at its best. In addition, Kingambit has no form of reliable recovery, so it often has to take hits before dishing out any damage, as well as being extremely reliant on Sucker Punch to maintain its ability to clean up weakened teams.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Kingambit can deal enormous damage to a multitude of foes, especially as very few can switch in reliably to check it. Various damage-boosting items can elevate its attacking power further, leaving lasting wounds that a teammate can take advantage of to clean up over the course of a match.

Setup Sweeper

: With access to Swords Dance, Kingambit can become a late-game wincon and cleave through even the sturdiest of walls, and it can pick off faster, frailer threats with Sucker Punch. While this requires great support from its teammates, it has a great payoff, as Kingambit can destroy entire teams if timed correctly.

Utility Attacker

: Kingambit can provide support if needed with moves such as Stealth Rock and Thunder Wave to disrupt foes while remaining strong due to its natural strength. Kingambit can also be a bulky attacker with either an Assault Vest or a Resistance Berry to maintain its presence and trade blows against problematic foes for a teammate to take out afterwards.

Common Items

Damage-boosting Items

: Damage-boosting items allow Kingambit to elevate its power further without being Choice-locked, which can be detrimental due to its low Speed. Black Glasses gives Kingambit's Dark-type moves more power to chip foes better. It can also use Expert Belt reliably, as it has good coverage.

: Kingambit can use Lum Berry if it doesn't have the flexibility to Terastallize into a type that can ignore a burn or paralysis.

: Air Balloon allows Kingambit to briefly ignore Ground-type coverage and is a potential lead option to disrupt problematic foes with Thunder Wave or to set up entry hazards. It can also force a foe to hit Kingambit with a weaker move, which it will then have to take an attack back.

Assault Vest

: If going up against specially based teams, Kingambit can use an Assault Vest to maintain its presence over the course of a match. It can also gain more power through either Supreme Overlord or Defiant to muscle through foes.

Resistance Berries

: If Kingambit does not expect to Terastallize in a matchup, Resistance Berries can be used against a problematic foe so that Kingambit can cripple back with either a strong attack of its own or status.

Choice Band

: Choice Band allows Kingambit to become almost impossible to switch into. This item can become more reliable against teams that lack Dark- and Steel-resistant foes.

Protective Pads

: If it goes up against a Pokemon with Flame Body or Static, Kingambit can use Protective Pads to ignore these abilities, especially as most of its primary moves and coverage make contact.

Custap Berry

: Custap Berry can allow Kingambit to surprise faster foes and fire off a status move or chip down a foe before it goes down.

Tera

Kingambit should always be your primary Tera Captain of choice if drafted. It is one of the top Tera Captains, as in a vacuum, it can Terastallize into any type it wants and still gain an advantage over foes provoking head-scratching during preparations. Tera Dark and Steel power up its STAB attacks further; Tera Fire can prevent it from being burned if it expects to go up against status users, and Tera Fairy, Flying, and Water are generally great defensive options to either gain a turn setting up or cripple a foe, as well as gaining necessary coverage to deal with foes with Tera Blast.

For this reason, it is often passed over for other great Tera Captains and often delayed for choosing other Pokemon to fulfil certain roles. Iron Defense variants of Zamazenta and Mega Medicham particularly benefit from Kingambit's ability to deal with the aforementioned Ghost-types, as they are difficult for them to break while also being able to cripple them with status effects. Access to Knock Off allows Kingambit to remove Heavy-Duty Boots from foes such as Alomomola, Zapdos, and Moltres for the benefit of hazard-setting teammates such as

Toxapex

, Ferrothorn, and

Mega Diancie

. Kingambit struggles against faster Pokemon such as Zamazenta, Great Tusk, Landorus-T, and

Kartana

. Partners such as Zapdos and Moltres can aid Kingambit by answering multiple of these Pokemon at once. Bulkier Pokemon such as

Melmetal

, Ting-Lu, and Toxapex are able to consistently answer Kingambit defensively, so Tapu Lele, Ogerpon-W, and

Raging Bolt

are greatly appreciated for their ability to immediately threaten these Pokemon. It is worth noting that a significant majority of the Kingambit checks mentioned are susceptible to losing their item to Knock Off.

With its unique typing, Kingambit serves as a powerful defensive tool against Pokemon such as

Giratina-O

lacking Will-O-Wisp,

Yveltal

,

Arceus

, and Choice Specs

Lunala

while also retaining a strong offensive presence thanks to Supreme Overlord, providing bulky offense and fat balance teams with a range of role compression courtesy of its valuable Ghost-, Psychic-, Dark- and Dragon-type resistances, access to priority, and even its ability to act as a late-game cleaner. Unfortunately, Kingambit lacks reliable recovery, which combined with its mediocre bulk relative to the tier, means it is prone to being overwhelmed easily.

Knock Off provides Kingambit with a source of utility by removing items such as Heavy-Duty Boots, preventing Pokemon such as

Ho-Oh

from reliably switching in. It also removes Life Orb from offensive Yveltal, weakening it severely, as well as removing items off other targets such as

Zygarde

, Arceus,

Ferrothorn

, and

Alomomola

, which can help teammates out in the long term. It helps Kingambit against stall teams too, since these structures are often reliant on items, such as the aforementioned Heavy-Duty Boots and

Chansey

's Eviolite, for their longevity. Especially considering that Kingambit often uses Sucker Punch for damage-dealing due to being outpaced by almost the entire metagame, having Knock Off to punish switch-ins against Kingambit is more useful on a regular basis than the slight and situational extra two hit damage provided from Kowtow Cleave.

Iron Head allows Kingambit to hit Fairy-types such as

Arceus-Fairy

and

Zacian-C

as well as Tera Fairy Zygarde and

Eternatus

. Sucker Punch is essential to make up for Kingambit's poor Speed, allowing it to remove foes such as

Ultra Necrozma

,

Deoxys-A

, and

Mega Mewtwo Y

before they can OHKO it instead. Low Kick can be considered to allow Kingambit to threaten

Arceus-Dark

, but it leaves Kingambit unable to threaten Arceus-Fairy or Tera Fairy Lunala.

Swords Dance allows Kingambit to punish the switches it forces, letting it wallbreak or be a strong late-game cleaner against more offensive teams, especially those lacking Zygarde or defensive Primal

Groudon

, such as against opposing hyper offense teams. It notably lets Kingambit OHKO Arceus-Fairy with Iron Head after Stealth Rock with no Supreme Overlord boosts and also OHKO Dragon Dance

Necrozma-DM

with Sucker Punch after a layer of Spikes. Alternatively, Pursuit is able to trap foes Kingambit switches into, such as Giratina-O and Choice-locked Lunala, wearing them down for offensive partners such as Primal Groudon. It is also good at trapping worn-down checks, such as Ho-Oh and support Arceus formes.

Supreme Overlord is preferred over Defiant due to its reliable damage boost, such as being able to 2HKO Arceus via Knock Off with 4 fainted allies. However, Defiant can be used, notably punishing Sticky Web structures and Defog attempts from Giratina-O and Ho-Oh. However, it is difficult to activate anywhere else, reducing Kingambit's damage overall.

The given EV spread maximises Attack and allows Kingambit to outspeed

Gothitelle

, preventing it from using Taunt or Charm reliably, while ensuring Tera Fairy variants cannot safely 1v1 Kingambit. This is especially useful for Pursuit variants that may be more passive into Gothitelle. The rest is dumped into HP, notably allowing Kingambit to survive Life Orb Yveltal's Heat Wave from full.

Kingambit's typing is essential to its use on a team, and thus doesn't often use Terastalization. Regardless, Tera Dark is its best option, removing Kingambit's Ground- and Fire-type weakness while weakening the damage taken from Fighting-type attacks. This helps Kingambit stomach Earthquake from Necrozma-DM, Ultra Necrozma, and Arceus, along with Heat Wave from Yveltal, while even letting it survive Deoxys-A's Life Orb Low Kick, allowing Kingambit to trade more effectively. Offensively, Tera Dark lets Kingambit OHKO

Marshadow

with Knock Off and Ultra Necrozma with Sucker Punch. It also makes Kingambit more threatening late-game, since it is able to OHKO Ho-Oh and defensive Primal Groudon with +2 Tera Dark Sucker Punch and five Supreme Overlord boosts. However, it should be emphasized that Kingambit relies on its Steel typing to resist attacks from Giratina-O and Arceus more easily and to not be as weak to Fairy-type attacks from Arceus-Fairy and Tera Fairy Lunala.

Swords Dance variants of Kingambit are primarily used late-game, being able to clean up weakened teams after its teammates have wrecked havoc. Due to its middling bulk, this will usually happen after a teammates has fainted, since this prevents Kingambit from getting worn down as badly while it sets up Swords Dance. The only time Kingambit should come out in the early-to-mid game is to tank resisted hits such as Lunala's Moongeist Beam and Giratina-O's Draco Meteor; otherwise, preserving Kingambit's HP by avoiding unnecessary damage from neutral hits is ideal to ensuring it can clean up effectively.

Pursuit variants of Kingambit are more aggressive than the Swords Dance counterpart; it's ideal to abuse double switches from teammates such as Primal Groudon, Zygarde, and

Arceus-Ground

, since all three of these typically invite in Giratina-O, from which Kingambit can easily Pursuit trap it, wearing it down for teammates to break through more easily afterwards. This variant, however, still doesn't appreciate being unnecessarily worn down, since otherwise it may be unable to reliably trap Pokemon such as Choice-locked Lunala and Marshadow and be forced to click Sucker Punch instead so as to not get KOed by their powerful attacks.

Defensive walls, such as Primal Groudon, Zygarde, and the more niche

Dondozo

, all easily stop Kingambit without significant boosts or Tera Dark to help it and threaten it with either STAB moves or, in Dondozo's case, Body Press. Giratina-O is able to handle all three of these, while it appreciates Kingambit switching into the Ghost- and Ice-type attacks Giratina-O doesn't like, such as against Choice Band variants of Marshadow and Chien Pao, notably preventing the former from mindlessly spamming its Ghost-type STAB moves. Kingambit also makes for a solid Yveltal and Arceus-Dark switch-in due to its resistances. Giratina-O is also able to remove hazards, assisting greatly in Kingambit's longevity, while relieving pressure off of Kingambit by checking Arceus itself.

Due to the utility of Knock Off, Kingambit works great with hazard setters such as defensive Primal Groudon in removing Heavy-Duty Boots off of targets like Ho-Oh and Alomomola, making wearing them down late-game easier for Kingambit itself or teammates. Primal Groudon is also able to check Zacian-C for Kingambit, while Kingambit in return harasses Giratina-O with Pursuit. There is also

Landorus-T

, which is similar to Giratina-O in soft-checking Arceus as well as Arceus-Ground, Zacian-C, and Dragon Dance Necrozma-DM. Its access to U-turn also allows it to bring in Kingambit safely against Pokemon it struggles with, such as Giratina-O and Yveltal, both of which can abuse Landorus-T's inability to threaten them back meaningfully.

Offensive partners such as Swords Dance Primal Groudon, Primal

Kyogre

, and Meteor Beam Eternatus, are all able to break through and wear down opposing teams, enabling Kingambit to sweep much more easily. The former is able to overwhelm Zygarde for Kingambit to muscle past it more easily afterwards, while the latter two are powerful special attackers capable of breaking through Zygarde, Ho-Oh and opposing Primal Groudon for Kingambit. In return, Kingambit provides a powerful revenge-killer and sweeper against opposing hyper offense teams that these three Pokemon don't like dealing with.
