Overview

: Ceruledge is a versatile and fearsome setup sweeper thanks to its great STAB combination, abilities, and decent bulk. Its signature move, Bitter Blade, allows it to simultaneously dish out damage and sustain its own HP, giving it great flexibility in choosing setup moves or coverage options. Terastallization makes Ceruledge truly shine, where it takes advantage of its abilities and solid bulk to set up and begin sweeping. Without use of Tera, Ceruledge is let down by its multitude of weaknesses to common offensive types. Its mediocre Speed often allows foes to attack it before it can get set up. However, when positioned well, it is capable of scoring multiple KOs or outright winning games on its own.

Common Roles

Weak Armor Sweeper

: Ceruledge can boost its Speed when physically attacked thanks to Weak Armor, allowing it to boost to +2 Speed and Attack on the same turn when using Swords Dance, enabling it to sweep through weakened teams with ease.

Bulky Setup

: Great special bulk and the ability Flash Fire allow Ceruledge many opportunities to set up with Bulk Up with the use of Tera. Bitter Blade sustains Ceruledge's HP, and Shadow Sneak patches up its low Speed to pick off faster attackers.

Leftovers

: Charcoal and Spell Tag boost the power of Ceruledge's STAB moves to help it secure KOs and recover more HP from Bitter Blade.

Niche Items

Pinch Berries

: Salac or Liechi Berry can be used either alone or in conjunction with Endure and Weak Armor to guarantee activation and a Speed boost to facilitate a sweep.

Air Balloon

: An Air Balloon helps Ceruledge set up vs Ground-type attacks and often forces coverage moves to break it. This often activates Ceruledge's Weak Armor in the process.

Tera

Ceruledge truly shines as a Tera Captain, and you should ideally draft it with the intention of making it one. Two amazing abilities, a multitude of setup moves, and a great offensive movepool make it an extremely robust Tera Captain. Flash Fire sets maintain an immunity to Fire with Tera Grass, Steel, or Bug, and Tera types like Fairy and Flying are effective to check Fire-type threats with strong secondary STAB moves, such as

Chi-Yu

and

Gouging Fire

. Weak Armor sets typically take advantage of Tera Ghost or Fire to maximize damage output from Ceruledge's STAB moves, Tera Fighting to boost the power of Close Combat, or another Tera type such as Fairy or Water to provide coverage it lacks via Tera Blast.

Draft Strategy

Ceruledge is heavily reliant on Tera to be an effective threat and should be drafted with this in mind. While it can still be a serviceable Weak Armor sweeper without Tera, Ceruledge being unable to Terastallize significantly hamstrings its flexibility.
