Strategy Comments

As a slower-paced offensive piece, Primarina excels when it's positioned to safely chip away at opponents. For instance, being alongside a redirection Pokemon allows it to unleash substantial chip damage with Hyper Voice. Due to its respectable bulk, Primarina can often continue using Hyper Voice until a Pokémon that poses a significant immediate threat, such as Rillaboom, enters the field.

The strategy with Primarina is to wait for the opportunity to establish a strong offensive position. Typically, this involves pivoting it around until it can come in and clean up effectively mid- or late-game.

As mentioned earlier, Rillaboom and Archaludon are significant threats; however, Sneasler effectively counters both. When facing opposing Sneasler or setup sweepers like Gholdengo or

Volcarona

, it's crucial to position Primarina correctly—either by placing it next to a redirector, so it can attack freely, or by utilizing other Pokemon to apply more immediate pressure. This is because Primarina is most effective when its consistent spread chip damage is sufficient to prevent it from being outpaced on offense.

Primarina is one of the strongest special wallbreakers in the tier, with an excellent typing that gives it opportunities to switch in and spam its powerful and reliable STAB moves. Primordial Sea boosts Weather Ball and lets Primarina check Desolate Land users such as

Overview

: Primarina is an excellent offensive threat thanks to its high Special Attack, enabling it to punch holes in opposing teams with its powerful STAB combination. It also possesses a nice defensive typing and special bulk, allowing it to check many special attackers and take on a role as a setup sweeper when using Calm Mind. Primarina can sometimes struggle to stay on the field due to its poor Speed and physical bulk, but it is still a dangerous offensive threat that must be carefully managed by your opponents.

Common Roles

Wallbreaker

: Few Pokemon can stomach the combination of Choice Specs-boosted Moonblast or Hydro Pump, allowing Primarina to severely dent or outright OHKO many Pokemon. Flip Turn allows Primarina to generate momentum for its teammates, taking advantage of most unfavorable matchups it encounters.

Setup Sweeper

: Primarina's impressive special bulk and excellent defensive typing allow it many opportunities to set up a Calm Mind, preparing it to sweep. Draining Kiss allows Primarina to stay healthy, and Psychic Noise helps break down bulky Pokemon that attempt to check Primarina. Liquid Voice turns Psychic Noise into a Water-type move if the coverage is not useful in the matchup, allowing more room for utility alongside Calm Mind, such as Substitute.

Defensive Pivot

: With defensive investment, Primarina can pivot into many attacks thanks to its great defensive typing and safely pivot to a teammate with a slow Flip Turn. Rocky Helmet can punish anticipated U-turn or Flip Turn, Assault Vest can help sponge special attacks, and Heavy-Duty Boots are useful on pivot sets if you anticipate switching Primarina into entry hazards.

Common Items

: Choice Specs boosts Primarina's Special Attack to absurd levels, allowing it to dish out incredibly powerful Moonblast and Hydro Pump.

Leftovers

: Leftovers allows Primarina to keep its HP high, supplement the healing gained by Draining Kiss, and is an effective pick for setup variants.

Heavy-Duty Boots

: Primarina's typing leaves it vulnerable to all forms of entry hazards, and Heavy-Duty Boots allows it to switch into the field uninhibited.

Assault Vest

: Primarina's already high Special Defense is boosted by Assault Vest, allowing it to check dangerous special attackers and retaliate with its powerful STAB attacks or safely Flip Turn to another teammate to handle the threat.

Niche Items

: Primarina's poor physical bulk does not allow it to take many strong physical hits, but its typing grants it numerous opportunities to punish resisted pivot moves, such as U-turn and Flip Turn, and other various weaker attackers with Rocky Helmet.

Tera

Primarina has an excellent defensive typing that grants it many opportunities to switch in to attacks during most games, which limits the need for defensive Terastallization. Additionally, its STAB combination already offers great coverage offensively, limiting the need for a different Tera Blast type. However, Primarina can still make good use of Tera both offensively and defensively. On offensive sets, Tera Water or Tera Fairy can push the damage output from its STAB moves even higher, enabling it to score more KOs that it wouldn't normally. Defensively, Primarina can make good use of many different typings, such as Tera Steel, Poison, or Fairy, to sponge hits or give it additional time to set up Calm Mind.

Draft Strategy

Primarina is a great choice for teams that are looking for a relatively self-sufficient wallbreaker that is capable of making extremely effective trades, thanks to the combination of its bulk, defensive typing, and offensive output. It is typically capable of securing KOs by itself, but it shines when placed on an offensive team that can safely pivot it in and provide entry hazard support to amplify its wallbreaking power.

: Primarina's wallbreaking capability enables many sweepers to take advantage of the holes it creates in opposing teams. Faster Pokemon like

Checks and Counters

Special Walls

: Dedicated special walls or some Assault Vest users are capable of checking Choice-locked Primarina and retaliating or pivoting to a teammate. Blissey and Toxapex, in particular, can shrug off Primarina's STAB moves and set up entry hazards or inflict status on Primarina. However, many special walls can be defeated by setup variants of Primarina, so they must be cautious when switching in.

Strong Physical Attackers

: Primarina's poor physical bulk leaves it liable to being quickly KOed by strong physical attackers. Many of the format's Dragon- or Fighting-types are often able to power through Primarina with their secondary STAB moves or coverage options before it can react.
